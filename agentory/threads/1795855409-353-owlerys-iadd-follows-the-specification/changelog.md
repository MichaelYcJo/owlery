# Changelog: Owlery's definition of IADD follows the IADD specification 0.1.0

Issue #353. Owlery and the AgenticSeal plugin each wrote a definition of IADD, and on 2026-11-28 the owner merged the two into one source document of the method's principles, in MichaelYcJo/iadd-workflows.

- **`docs/iadd.md` states Owlery as a product built on IADD.** The IADD specification (MichaelYcJo/iadd-workflows) now holds the method's principles, and Owlery takes from it what serves Owlery. The document no longer restates the concept, the principles or the names set aside. It gives Owlery's choices, what Owlery holds of each of the eleven principles and where, Owlery's own names, and Owlery's own record forms in full: the root and its configuration, a work item's files and closing records, the record blocks, the three states as Owlery reads them, and the trailers. Where Owlery's code still leaves a gap that matters to Owlery, the row names its issue (#139, #286, #363, #365, #367, #369).
- **The Korean documents name an inspection's states 합격, 효력을 잃음 and 합격 아님**, the specification's names, in place of 검수 통과, 검수 실효 and 검수 없음.
- **Three sentences that still named the check `seal`, or left out what #354's move changed, are fixed (#362).**
- **The version is 0.2.1.**
