# Changelog: a click on a sidebar line survives a redraw

Issue #242. A click on a work item's line in the sidebar sometimes opened nothing. Each list the daemon sends redraws the sidebar, the screen in front and some dialogs, and the redraw replaces every line and button with a new one. Where that happened between the press of the mouse button and its release, the pressed line had left the page, and Chromium sends no click at all for such a press. On a machine where agents work, the daemon sends lists often, so the click was lost often.

- **A click on a line or a button still does what it does when the daemon's lists arrive between its press and its release.** While the mouse's main button is held down in the window, the window takes each `inbox`, `accounts` and `machine` message at once and raises its notifications. It waits to draw what the message changed until the button is released, and then draws once, after the click has reached the element that was pressed. A release of the main button ends the wait even while another button is still held down, and a cancelled press and a window that loses the focus end it too. A press of another button alone makes nothing wait. This covers the sidebar, the inbox and the other list screens, the summary, a work item's view, the dialogs that name what the daemon lists, and the Accounts and Machine dialogs, whose rows and buttons an `accounts` or a `machine` message draws again.
- **The `accounts` and `machine` messages draw the summary, the archived screen and the open dialogs as an `inbox` does.** Before, they drew only the sidebar, the list in front and the view.
- **The built-app cases that opened a view by clicking its sidebar line again and again until it showed click it once.** A new case in `apps/desktop/test/seal-back-view.test.ts` holds a press on a line while the window takes an `inbox`, releases it on the same point, and expects the work item's view, and then the change the `inbox` carried.
