**Constraints.**

- The daemon runs no Python (`docs/architecture.md` *What we take from AgenticSeal*), so the daemon and the check hold the rule in two languages. *Scope* item 11 of `frame/design.md` holds them to one answer for the histories it lists.
- The page's style writes no filter of its own, and every token stands in every block of `themes.css` and in both editions of `docs/design.md` (`apps/desktop/test/themes.test.ts`).
- No message carries a session's id, and no field names a vendor.
- No case reaches GitHub or starts a real `claude`.

**The failure scenario of the chosen approach.** In six months a team keeps its branches up to date with the base by merging it, and each such merge after a seal breaks the seal: the line waits, a notification arrives, and a round, a gate and a sealer run again before the pull request can merge. The promise asks for exactly that, and it is the cost a person meets first. The second failure: the order's function and the check drift, and a line shows a lit seal on a head whose check fails. Scenario 9 holds the two together for the histories it lists and for no other. A merge of the base whose commits leave the tree as it was reads `broken` in the daemon and passes the check, since the rounds arm skips the base's own commits. A verdict block written by hand whose judge is one of its builders reads `sealed` in the daemon and fails the check, since `stepOwed` does not read those lines; the daemon writes no such block. The third: the head reading costs one git call for each round record and eight more at every move of a head, each about 20 milliseconds here (`frame/design.md` *Grounding*), so a daemon with many open work items and long chains reads slowly after a fetch that moves many branches. `frame/questions.md` Q4 names what measures it. The fourth: a person who goes on working after a seal gets one notification at the first commit, and mutes the work item. The fifth: a sealer that judges the changed part stands on the earlier run's audit, so a defect the earlier run missed is not met again by the new seal.
