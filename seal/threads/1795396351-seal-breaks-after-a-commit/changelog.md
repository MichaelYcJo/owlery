# Changelog: a commit after the seal breaks it, in the app and on the pull request

Issue #44. A sealed branch kept saying sealed after a later commit. The chain stored the state `sealed` and kept it whatever landed on the branch, the app lit no seal at all, and the pull request check passed a merge commit made after the seal, even one that changed a source file.

- **A work item's seal is read from its branch's newest commit.** Owlery's background process, the daemon, reads the work item's records as that commit holds them, each time the branch's head moves and when the daemon starts. The seal holds where only the gate's record, `guarantee.md` and `trail/refusals.md` changed after the sealed commit, and no review round, gate or sealer is owed. A file you have not committed, what a pane prints and anything the daemon stored decide nothing, so a restart changes no seal.
- **A commit after the seal breaks it, wherever it is made.** That is a commit in a pane of the app, in your own shell, or in another checkout that moves the branch. A merge of the base branch is such a commit, and so is a reversal or an answer you give, which Owlery commits. A commit that changes only the gate's record, `guarantee.md` or `trail/refusals.md` leaves the seal as it is.
- **The seal's place is lit where the seal holds, and parted in two where it is broken.** This is on the work item's line, in the inbox's preview and at the foot of its view. A broken seal is the seal's drawing split along one slanted break, with the wax's red taken out. The place says "Sealed at 1a2b3c4" or "Seal broken, sealed at 1a2b3c4" to a pointer and to a screen reader, and the preview and the view's foot show those words beside it.
- **A broken seal makes the work item's line wait.** The line stands under "Needs you", its second row reads "Seal broken", and a notification is raised when the seal breaks. A pane that works or waits, a running or stopped chain, a park, a call waiting for you and a merged pull request still say more than the seal. A broken seal says more than an open pull request's result, and no notification of that result is raised while the seal is broken. A sealed work item with no pull request finishes, and its second row reads "Sealed".
- **The view's foot says why the seal broke, and offers "Get a new seal".** Under the words of the seal's place stands one sentence, for example "`src/app.ts` changed after the sealed commit `1a2b3c4`." The button runs what the work item owes: a review round on the commits after the last round, the broad gate, and the sealer. The sealer of a new seal is handed the part that changed since the broken seal, and its verdict is still a verdict on the whole branch. Where the branch's pull request is open, the new head is pushed and the seal section of its body is replaced.
- **The sealer's place of the approval line reads "Sealed" only where the seal holds at the branch's newest commit.** Where a later commit broke it, the place waits with "Seal broken", and the sentence that says why stands under the places.
- **On the pull request, the check `Owlery seal` judges the same records.** A ready pull request whose seal a later commit broke gets a failed check titled "Seal broken: a commit landed after 1a2b3c4". The check now compares the tree of the sealed commit with the tree of the head commit, and reads the commits of the base that a merge brought after the seal. So a merge commit after the seal fails it, even one whose change the sealed tree already held, and so does a change made inside one. The app and the check judge alike what lands after a seal the app gave. Records that no chain of the app commits, written by hand or by another tool, can be judged apart: the check reads no `decisions.md`, for one. The seal section of the pull request's body now says that a seal binds the commit it names and that the check says whether it holds at the head commit.
- **No commit pushed after a seal has reached GitHub yet.** Every test ran the check on a repository on the same Mac and posted to a server on the same Mac. Whether GitHub runs the check again at such a push and shows the failed check is measured by the first pull request Owlery opens on GitHub.
- **After an update, the seal is read once the daemon restarts.** A daemon started by the build before this one sends no seal, and the window shows "No seal yet" for each work item. A chain that an earlier build of this release stored as sealed is read as no chain, and the next press of "Get a seal" goes on from the committed records.
- **For a client of the daemon:** wherever the daemon sends a work item whose branch it has read since it started, and in the last look, the work item carries `seal`: `null`, or `state` (`sealed` or `broken`), `commit` (the sealed commit) and `why` (the sentence of a broken seal, `null` where the seal holds). A work item whose branch the daemon has not read since it started carries no `seal`, and a client reads its absence as no seal, as a client of an earlier build does. A chain's `state` is `running`, `parked`, `stopped` or `rested`. `rested` says that the chain's run ended and owes no stage, and `sealed` is gone. No message is added.
