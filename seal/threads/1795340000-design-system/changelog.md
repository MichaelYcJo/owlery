# Changelog: the window's design system

Issue #130. The window had two looks, Night desk and Letter, and no document said how a part of it is drawn. Each change drew its own buttons, lines and menus, and the owner checked each one by hand.

- **An updated window opens in Night watch.** Night watch is the theme a first run opens in: a black ground and a warm white ink from the app's emblem, with the owl's yellow eye as the one colour that says a work item needs the person. A window that opened in Night desk opens in Night watch's dark appearance after the update, one that opened in Letter opens in its light appearance, and one that followed the system still follows it.
- **Desk is the earlier look.** A person who wants the window as it was chooses Desk in the theme's control at the sidebar's foot. Desk's dark appearance is the earlier Night desk and its light appearance is the earlier Letter, each with its two quieter inks made stronger, as the next item says.
- **Every word of the window can be read, in every theme.** The quiet inks were too close to the ground under them: a hint, a count, an age, a commit's hash and the words "Finished" and "Asleep" stood at about 3 to 1. Every word now stands at 4.5 to 1 or more against the ground it is drawn on, a selected line's and one under the pointer among them, and a state's mark and the ring of the keyboard's focus at 3 to 1 or more. `docs/design.md` states that floor as a principle, and a case of the suite computes it for every theme in both appearances.
- **The keyboard's focus shows on the main button.** Its ring was drawn inside the button, in Night watch in the button's own colour, so nothing showed which button had the focus. The ring now stands outside the button's edge. On the tab of a pane the ring and the close mark take the terminal's inks, so both show on the terminal's dark ground in a light appearance too.
- **The window has three themes, and each has a dark and a light appearance.** Night watch, Register and Desk. Register draws the window as a ruled book of records: square corners, a rule under each line of the inbox and under each commit, changed file and fact of a work item, and facts in the fixed-width typeface. The sidebar's foot holds two controls where it held one: one chooses the theme, and the other chooses Dark, Light or Match the system.
- **A state's mark is the theme's own, and none is a speech bubble.** Night watch draws the owl's eye: an open eye needs the person, a closed eye is finished, a crescent is asleep. Register draws a form's boxes, and Desk a filled disc, a ticked circle and a crescent. The mark of a wait was a chat bubble before, and the desktop has no chat.
- **Two states have new words.** A work item whose agent waits says "Needs you" where it said "Waiting for you": in its line of the inbox, in a view and in a notification, and in every sentence that names the state. The inbox's head and the summary say "1 needs you, 2 working", a change's sentence says "Needs you.", the summary's hint says "Enter opens the first that needs you.", and the sidebar's bell says "2 need you. Show them alone." A work item with no agent says "Asleep" where it said "No agent".
- **`appearance.json` holds a theme and an appearance.** The app reads a file of the earlier form and writes the new form at the next choice. An earlier version of the app that reads a file of the new form opens in Night desk.
- **`docs/design.md` states the window's style.** It holds the principles, the three themes, every value a theme draws with, the forms of the parts and their states, the voice of the window's words, and what the window stays clear of. `docs/product.md` now lets a theme change the form of edges, rules and marks, and never the layout, the words, the seal's drawing or the wax's red. A case of the suite fails where a value of the style sheet and the document differ, in either edition.
- **`pnpm run screenshots` writes the window's screenshots.** Run in `apps/desktop`, it starts the built app in the background on invented work items, one in each state, and writes the inbox, a work item's view, a menu and a dialog as PNG files, in Night watch's two appearances and in the other themes where it is asked for them. A pull request that changes the window carries the screenshots of what it changed, and its review reads them against `docs/design.md`.
