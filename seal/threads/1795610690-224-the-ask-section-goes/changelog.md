# Changelog: the view's "Ask" section goes

Issue #224. A work item's view had an "Ask" section whose field started a fresh headless session in the work item's folder. That session knew nothing of the work, held Claude Code's default tools, and had each call it wanted to make wait for the person on a card in the view. Nothing else in the window or the daemon started such a session.

- **The view has no "Ask" section.** Its heading, its field, its account menu, its button and its empty text are gone. The lines it held stay at the view's foot, above the seal's place, with no heading: the line of the broad gate the work item's chain ran last, with "Stop" and "Show output", and a line for each session of the chain's stages. A screen reader names the list "The daemon's chain". Where the list holds no line, as for a work item whose panes run the owl chain, the foot holds the seal's place alone.
- **No session waits for the person to allow a call.** Every session of a chain's stage has a role, and its role's grant allows or refuses each call; the refresh of an account's usage, which has no role, holds no tool. The card with "Allow" and "Refuse", the line in the inbox that waited on a call, and the notification of a call are gone.
- **A session is named by its stage.** The dialogs before an archive, a repository's removal and the daemon's end, and the Machine dialog's queue, name a session by the stage it runs, as in "Proofer, round 2", where they named the prompt after "Asked:". The refusal to remove an account, or to refresh its usage, while a session runs under it names the session by its role and its work item, as in `the proofer's session in "Fix the login redirect"`.
- **The machine's queue has two ranks.** A stage of a chain that already ran a stage starts first, then a chain's first stage.
- **The protocol between the window and the daemon changes.** The messages `ask`, `asked` and `answerPermission` and a work item's `permissions` are gone. Until the daemon restarts on the new build, a window of the earlier build gets no answer from its "Ask" field, and a window of the new build ignores the `permissions` an earlier daemon sends. A last look an earlier daemon wrote still reads.
- **The screenshots show a failed broad gate.** The scene's view work item is stored with a chain that stopped where its broad gate failed, and its shot shows what the gate's command printed. The Machine dialog's shot shows a resumed chain's stage that waits for the machine.

The rewrite of `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/design.md` and `docs/product.md` and their Korean editions is left to the settle, which writes the operations of this work item's `frame/design.md` *Rule changes*.
