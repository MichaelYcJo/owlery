# Implementation Plan: 1795610690-224-the-ask-section-goes

## Technical context

The tree as read at `d38f77a4`.

The window starts a headless session from one place, the "Ask" form of a work item's view (`apps/desktop/src/renderer/work-item-view.ts:1362-1369`, `index.html:781-789`), through the client message `ask` (`apps/desktop/src/shared/protocol.ts:928`), which the daemon handles at `apps/desktop/src/daemon/daemon.ts:1498-1532` by calling `beginHeadless` with the client's role or none. The daemon's chain starts its stages through the same `beginHeadless`, always with a role (`daemon.ts:3211-3220`). A session with no role is the only source of a call that waits for the person (`role-grants.ts:136-137`). The calls it holds reach the window as `WorkItem.permissions` (`daemon.ts:2800`), and the window reads them for a line's state, its preview, its notifications and the card in the section's list (`view.ts:125-126`, `180`, `262`, `285`, `825-828`, `1474`, `1760`, `3286`; `work-item-view.ts:777-785`).

The section's list (`#headless`) draws every headless session of the work item and the daemon's chain's newest broad gate (`view.ts:3285-3368`, `work-item-view.ts:788-900`). It is redrawn only where its key differs (`headlessKey`), so the keyboard stays on a button through every `inbox`. This frame keeps that list where it stands, at the right column's foot above the seal's place, and keeps its drawing code. It takes the form away, gives the list an id and an accessible name of its own, and hides it while it holds no line.

The cases reach the daemon three ways today. A case in the `unit` project builds a `Daemon` in its own process and talks to it over a socket (`test/daemon.test.ts:57`, `test/accounts.test.ts:378`, `test/chain-run.ts:324`), and already calls `Daemon#startChain` directly where no message exists (`test/chain-run.ts:101-106`). A built-app case reaches the daemon of a built app or a built daemon over its socket (`test/machine-dialog.test.ts:378-391`, `test/role-bundle.test.ts:301-360`, `test/screenshots.test.ts:733`). The screenshot run also types into the view (`test/screenshots.test.ts:715-724`). The first kind moves to a public method of `Daemon`. The second and third reach a role session through a parked chain the case stores and answers, which the daemon resumes (`daemon.ts:1276`, `daemon.ts:2989-3000`), the path milestone 3 builds on.

Failure scenario of the chosen approach, in six months: the built-app cases of the Machine dialog's queue and of the role bundle now depend on a parked chain's records and on `whyNoChain`'s conditions (`daemon.ts:3022-3053`). A change to the chain's record grammar or to what a resumed chain owes, such as issue #235's stage prompts or milestone 3's framer stage, can turn those cases red far from what they test, and the person reading the failure sees a chain that did not resume rather than a Machine dialog that drew wrongly. The cases' helpers that store the chain sit beside the screenshot run's (`test/screenshots.ts`, `test/chain-run.ts`), so a change to the records meets them in one place.

## Operational impact

- The protocol between the window and the daemon changes: `ask`, `asked` and `answerPermission` go, and `WorkItem` loses `permissions`. A window of the new build connected to a daemon of the earlier build ignores the `permissions` it sends. A window of the earlier build connected to the new daemon sends `ask` from its form, the daemon drops it (`daemon.ts:1701`), and that window's field keeps its prompt and waits with no answer (`work-item-view.ts:1364-1369`). Both last until the daemon restarts on the new build.
- A look file (`last-look.ts`) written by the earlier daemon holds `permissions` on each work item, and the new daemon reads it as before, less that field (design, Scope item 4).
- No migration, no new environment variable, no new dependency. This pull request moves no version: #237 moves it to 0.0.5.
