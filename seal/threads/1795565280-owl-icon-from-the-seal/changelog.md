# Changelog: the app icon is the seal's owl

Issue #201. People read the owl of the app's icon as a skull. The owner compared drawn candidates and chose owl L: the owl of the red wax seal, taken out of its wax and drawn in warm white on the black rounded square.

- **The app's icon is owl L.** The Dock and both READMEs show the seal's owl without the feathers on its forehead and round its eyes, with each line 1.8 times as thick. The icon drawn for 32 pixels and under is the same owl without the feathers on its chest and wings, with each line 3 times as thick as the seal's.
- **The earlier icon is kept in the repository.** The three files of the emblem that releases 0.0.1 through 0.0.3 showed stand in `apps/desktop/assets/icon/earlier-2026-11-19/`, and the build ships none of them. A case of `apps/desktop/test/emblem.test.ts` holds that the built app's icon is the new one and that no file of the build is a file of the earlier icon.
- **The small owl on the line of a sealed work item does not change.** It is AgenticSeal's drawing for that size, and owl L shows no eyes at 18 pixels.
