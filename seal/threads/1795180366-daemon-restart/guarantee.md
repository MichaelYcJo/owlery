### What the wait could hide

A person could meet the state the gate's run met for only a moment. The menu's click sends its choice once the page has loaded (`main.ts#tellDaemonMenu`), and the socket's `ready` can come a moment later. In that interval the dialog opens with its list hidden and its button on. The redraw at `ready` then names the sessions. The defect a person could meet, a dialog that names no session while sessions run, needs three things at once: the choice falls inside a page load, the person presses the button before `ready` lands, and the keyboard, which starts on Cancel, has already left Cancel. The dialog's sentence still says that every running session ends. This is no claim of the changelog or the body that fails, and it is no lasting state. It is noted here for the orchestrator, not a reason to withhold: *Scope* item 5 hides the list where the window holds no running session, and before `ready` the window holds none.
