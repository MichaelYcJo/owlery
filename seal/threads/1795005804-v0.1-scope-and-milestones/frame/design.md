**Why this order.** Four grounds, and the order they reject.

1. The process comes right after the shell, and the back of the seal comes with the process. The ground holds three statements, in this order. First, the shell comes first because the AgenticSeal plugin already shows the value of the process inside Claude Code, so what Owlery has to establish first is an app a person hands work to and steps away from. This sentence moves here from the old opening paragraph of `docs/architecture.md` *Build order*. Second, the process is built on the skeleton milestone 1 connects, for the reason *Build order* gives. The ground cites that reason by a link and does not state it, because *Scope* item 8 keeps it in `docs/architecture.md`. Third, the back of the seal shows early, in the user's own numbers, whether the seal is worth its cost. This statement moves here from the bullet on the back of the seal.
2. The person's review tools read the seal. A hand commit breaks it, a line comment reaches the crafter as a finding, and the merge button asks whether it holds (`docs/product.md` *File explorer and editor*). Built before the seal, each is built once without it and again with it.
3. The process is the part whose structure can still change. Whether role sessions may run headless is a judgment made again when its grounds change, and the document links to its row in `docs/architecture.md` *Open decisions* without restating those grounds. A move to terminals read through hooks costs less while less is built on the daemon.
4. Daily use does not wait for the workspace milestone. Milestone 1 carries what makes a terminal usable all day, and until milestone 2 Owlery's own PRs are sealed by the AgenticSeal plugin, so the first done criterion holds from the first PR the project's own work opens.

The process is split into milestones 2 and 3 because "Get a seal" is the smaller half and needs only a branch that exists, and it produces the first numbers on the back of the seal. The rejected order, the workspace before the process, is stated in one sentence with the rework of ground 2.
