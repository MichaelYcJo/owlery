The judgments the issue left open that the tree and Anthropic's published documents answered, each with where its grounds stand. Nobody needs to reopen them.

- How an account is added and stored with no token in Owlery's hands: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows on a config folder per account and on a long-lived token.
- Whether running the CLI's own sign-in from the app is a login that Owlery offers: `frame/design.md` *Grounding*, the entry on Anthropic's Legal and compliance page.
- How the nearest setting is found: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows on one selected account for the host and on where a setting is kept.
- Which parts of the config folder are the person's: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows on linking every entry and on `settings.json`. Q1 below puts the one part that departs from the issue's words to the person.
- How a running session changes account: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows on resuming a moved pane and on issue #61.
- The issue's open decision on reading accounts Orca or another tool made: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on Orca's index.
- The issue's open decision on Codex and other agents: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on Codex.
- How an account's folder combines with the bundle, the head and the settings file of a pane that carries the owl chain (#181): `frame/design.md` *Scope* items 7 and 14, and *Grounding*, the entries on #181's branch and on the tree after #181 was merged.
- Which of this work and #181 merges first, which side ports what, and when each phase is built: `frame/plan.md` *Overlap with the release*, and *Alternatives considered*, the rows on building phase 2 before #181 is merged and on holding every phase.
- Whether the watch of a pane's session log (#182) follows an account's folder: `frame/design.md` *Grounding*, the entry on `#followLog`, and the entry that the watch is as it was after #181 was merged.
- Which config folder each part of the daemon reads under System default, where the login shell's, the daemon's own environment's and the home's can differ: `frame/design.md` *Data & interfaces*, the person's own config folder, and `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the two rows on reading the daemon's environment.
- Whether the window asks the choice of a chain before a pane whose own account enables the AgenticSeal plugin while its repository's does not. It does not: `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 14 and *Grounding*, the entry on the window's question, and `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on sending the window a reading for each account. This was the row Q7 until #181 was merged, and the tree now answers it.
- Whether work may move to another account at a limit: `frame/design.md` *Scope*, out of scope, "Moving work to another account when a limit is near". The daemon never chooses an account, so the question of Anthropic's terms does not arise for anything Owlery does by itself.
