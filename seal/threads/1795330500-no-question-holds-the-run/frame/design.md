# Design: under `agentic`, no question holds the run

The ticket is issue #119. A person who hands several work items over and leaves found the run stopped by a question asked in the middle, and found nowhere the decisions the orchestrator made in place of asking. The issue asks that under the routing answer `agentic` the questions are asked once, that a later question is decided from the code and the policy and shown in the inbox as a decision made without asking, with its grounds, and that only what the code and the policy cannot answer and the session cannot give itself parks the one work item it belongs to while the others go on.

This work builds the part of that which stands on today's tree: the rule, written into the policy documents in both languages in this pull request, the daemon's reading and writing of the decisions and the questions in a work item's records, and their place on the inbox line, in the work item's view and in the summary. The work item's records are the one source of both, in the format #40 builds (*Grounding*, the facts of the reframe), and the daemon keeps no copy of them. The daemon runs no stage and no orchestrator session today, so the callers that park a work item and record a decision are named for the issues that build them (*Scope* item 16).

## Scope

**In.** Items 1 to 5 are the decisions, items 6 to 11 the park, items 12 to 15 what both share, item 21 the policy documents' text, item 22 the commit of a person's word, and item 23 what the part "What the daemon could do beyond the issue" takes in. The exact shapes and texts are in *Data & interfaces*.

1. **A decision is a block of the work item's record.** It is a `seal` block of `decisions.md` in the work item's directory, as #40's `decision-record.ts` reads it: an id, a kind, what was decided, its grounds, who decided where it is said, and a state. The kinds are the ones the issue names: a route, a default taken, a scope chosen, and a question the tree answered. The states are `decided`, `reversed`, `applied` with a commit and `carried` with a home, and a block that is not `decided` holds the person's message. The daemon keeps no copy of a decision.
2. **The daemon reads each work item's record from its worktree.** It finds the work item's directory with `workItemDirectory` and reads `decisions.md` with `readDecisionRecord`, whether or not the file is committed. It reads in the pass that reads the work items' branches, before it waits on git for any of them, at a hello, and directly after a write of its own, and it sends an `inbox` where what it read differs from what it last sent. A work item with no directory, or with no `decisions.md`, has no decision and no question. A block the reader refuses, and a fence the reader names as not read, is not read as a decision or a question: the daemon sends its line and the reader's reason with the work item and decides nothing on it. A `decisions.md` that stands and cannot be read is one such line, at line 1, that says why. The daemon reads an archived work item's record at the archive, at its restore and at its own start, and not in the pass, since it reads git for no archived work item.
3. **`Daemon#decided` writes a decision block and tells every client.** It is a method of the daemon's own code, as `Daemon#agentEvent` is. It writes one `decided` block through `withDecision` and `writeRecord`, reads the record again, and sends an `inbox`. It changes nothing of any session and of any other work item, and raises no notification. It refuses a work item the daemon does not keep, a work item with no directory, a kind that is no `DecisionKind`, and an empty `says`, `grounds` or `by`, and then writes nothing and sends nothing. A `by` left out or null writes no `by:` line.
4. **A person reverses a decision with a message.** The client message `reverseDecision` names the work item, the decision's id and the message. The daemon writes the reversal into the block through `withReversal`, so the block reads `state: reversed` with the message in `reversal:`, and answers `done`. It answers `error`, and changes no file, for an empty message, for an id the record holds no decision of, for a decision that is not `decided`, and for a work item whose worktree holds no directory, with "Work item N has no record of decisions.".
5. **The inbox shows the decisions on their work item, and the work item's view lists them.** A line says how many decisions stand, a standing one being one whose state is `decided`. The preview and the view list every decision under the head "Decided without asking", the newest first, each with its kind, what was decided, its grounds, who decided where the block says it, its age, and for a decision that is not `decided` its state's words and the person's message: "Reversed", "Applied in" with the commit, or "Carried to" with the home. In the view each standing decision has a control "Reverse" that opens a dialog holding the field for the message (the view's right column is drawn again at every `inbox`, so a field inside the list would lose the keyboard and Korean composition) and sends `reverseDecision`. No decision changes a line's state or its group. Under the list the view names each entry of the work item's `unread` (item 12), by its line in `decisions.md` and the reader's reason. The count stands on the line for as long as its work item is in the inbox. An archive takes the work item off the inbox, and its decisions with it: the daemon lists an archived work item under `archived` alone, so no line of the inbox and no count of the summary holds them. They stay listed where the archived work item is shown, on its line and in its preview on the archived screen, and in the view again once it is restored. Taking the count off the line when the work item's pull request merges is #43's, since no work item knows its pull request before #43.
6. **A question a work item is parked with is a block of the same record.** It holds an id, what is asked, the grounds, who asks where it is said, its options, each with what it implies, its default where it has one, and a state, `open` or `answered` with the person's answer. A work item is parked while its record holds an `open` question.
7. **The daemon keeps when it first read each block in its state, and the inbox's ages are counted from that.** The record holds no clock. `record-times.json` in the run folder holds, under each work item's id and each block's id, the state the daemon last read the block in and the daemon's clock when it first read it so. A block whose state changed gets a new time, and an entry whose id the record no longer holds is dropped. The file serves the ages alone: where it is lost, each age starts again at the daemon's next read, and nothing else changes. The file is written whole after a read or a drop that changed it, once for each pass, and read at the daemon's start, as `last-look.json` is.
8. **`Daemon#park` writes a work item's questions as `open` blocks and tells every client.** It is a method of the daemon's own code. It writes one block for each question through `withQuestion`, in one write of the file, reads the record again and sends an `inbox`. It starts nothing, stops nothing and refuses nothing in any work item, the parked one among them: a pane and an `ask` in a parked work item are served as before, and a park of a work item that is already parked adds its questions. Which of `Daemon#decided` and `Daemon#park` a caller uses is the rule's (item 21): a caller parks only for what the code and the policy cannot answer and the session cannot give itself, such as a permission, a credential or an approval, and records every other late question as a decision. The method itself does not judge what it is given. It refuses a work item the daemon does not keep, an archived one, one with no directory, a call with no question, an empty `by`, a question with an empty text or empty grounds, two options of one question with one label, and a default that names no option of its question, and then writes nothing.
9. **A person's answer in the app ends the park.** The client message `answerQuestions` carries one answer for each `open` question of the record. An answer is an option's label or the person's own words. The daemon writes every answer through `withAnswer` in one write of the file, so each block reads `state: answered` with its `answer:`, and answers `done`. It answers `error`, and changes no file, where the work item holds no `open` question, where an `open` question has no answer in the message, where the message answers an id that is no `open` question of the record, for an empty answer, and for a work item whose worktree holds no directory, with "Work item N has no record of decisions.". The answered blocks stay in the record, where the stage that goes on reads them.
10. **A parked work item's line waits for the person and says its question.** Its state is `waiting`, whatever its panes say, so it stands in the inbox's first group, is counted in "waiting for you", and is what the summary's main control opens. Its second row says the first `open` question's text, followed by " (and 1 more question)" or " (and N more questions)" where the record holds more `open` ones. Its age is counted from the earliest time of item 7 among its `open` questions. Once every question is answered, the line is read from the work item's panes as before.
11. **The view of a parked work item holds the questions and takes the answers, and the window raises a notification at a park.** Under the head "Waiting for your answer" the view shows every `open` question at once, each with who asked where the block says it, its grounds, its options, what each implies, and a field for another answer. A question's default is chosen when the view opens. "Send answers" sends `answerQuestions`, and every control is reached by the keyboard. After the answer the section's head is "Answered", and it lists each answered question with its answer, until the next look: an answered question is listed while the look the window holds, which is the look its `ready` carried, does not hold that question as answered for the work item, and while the window holds no look. Once that look holds every answered question as answered, the view draws no section. The blocks themselves stay in the record (item 9). The notification is titled with the work item's title and says "Waiting for your answer". None is raised for a muted work item, for the work item whose view is in front, or for a work item the window already held as parked.
12. **A work item carries what the daemon read of its record over the protocol.** `WorkItem` gains `decisions`, `questions` and `unread`, which holds the blocks the reader refused and the fences it names as not read, its notices, in the order of their lines. A client reads a work item without them, as a daemon of an earlier build sends it, as one with no decision, no question and nothing unread.
13. **The look is what the person has seen, and a look written before these fields is still a look.** `lookOf` copies `decisions`, `questions` and `unread`, so the look holds, under each work item's id, the ids and the states the person saw. It is the daemon's state and stands in no record. A look's file whose work items lack the fields is read with none on each, as the fields that came with the repositories are read.
14. **The summary counts the decisions apart from the work items that wait.** A work item is changed since the look where its record holds a decision whose id the look does not hold for that work item, and where its line's state differs, which a park and an answer both change. Its line in the summary adds "1 decision made without asking." or "N decisions made without asking." for the decisions the look does not hold. The summary's sentence keeps its count by state and adds, after a full stop, "1 decision made without asking." or "N decisions made without asking.", the sum over the changed work items, where the sum is above zero. The summary reads the work items the daemon lists as not archived, so a decision of an archived work item is in no line and in no count of it.
15. **The record lives and goes with the work item's worktree, and the daemon's times go with the work item.** A delete removes the worktree and the record in it, and the daemon then drops the work item's entries of `record-times.json`, as it does at the removal of a repository. Where a new work item is given its id, the daemon drops what that file holds under the id, and under no other, so a work item made after `repositories.json` was lost and its ids began again takes no earlier work item's times. The daemon's start drops nothing. An archived work item keeps its worktree and so its record, and is listed under `archived` with what the daemon read of it.

22. **The daemon commits a person's word in the record where no session of the work item is working.** After a `reverseDecision` or an `answerQuestions` wrote the file, the daemon commits `decisions.md` alone, in the work item's worktree, where no pane of the work item is in the state `working` and no headless session of it is working. It stages that path alone and commits that path alone, with the person's git identity and the repository's hooks, and it pushes nothing. What else is staged or changed in the worktree stays as it was. Where a session of the work item is working, the daemon leaves the file written and uncommitted, and the session's or the stage's own commit carries it. Where the commit fails, the file stays written, the request is still answered `done`, and the daemon logs git's words. `Daemon#decided` and `Daemon#park` commit nothing: the stage that calls them commits the record with its own commit.

## Data & interfaces

All in `apps/desktop`. No field names a vendor or a role's model. The file's format is #40's, and no module of this work spells a key of it: every read and every write of a record goes through `src/daemon/decision-record.ts`.

**`src/shared/protocol.ts`.**

```ts
/** Why the orchestrator decided without asking: it chose a route, took a default, chose a scope, or found the question answered in the tree. */
export type DecisionKind = "route" | "default" | "scope" | "answered";

/** Where a decision stands: as made, reversed by the person, taken in by a stage, or carried to another home after the merge. */
export type DecisionState = "decided" | "reversed" | "applied" | "carried";

/** One decision made for a work item in place of asking the person, as the daemon read it in the work item's record. */
export interface Decision {
  /** The block's id, given once over the work item's record. Two work items may each hold the same id. */
  id: number;
  kind: DecisionKind;
  /** What was decided. */
  says: string;
  /** Why, with the coordinate the decision stands on. */
  grounds: string;
  /** Who decided, where the block says it. */
  by: string | null;
  state: DecisionState;
  /** The person's message, where the state is not `decided`. */
  reversal: string | null;
  /** The commit that took the reversal in where `applied`, the home it went to where `carried`, and null otherwise. */
  landed: string | null;
  /** The daemon's clock when it first read the block in its state, or null where it has none. */
  since: number | null;
}

/** One question a work item is or was parked with, as the daemon read it in the work item's record. The work item is parked while one of its questions has no answer. */
export interface Question {
  /** The block's id, of the same count as a decision's. */
  id: number;
  asks: string;
  /** Why the code and the policy do not answer it and the session cannot give it itself. */
  grounds: string;
  /** Who asks, where the block says it. */
  by: string | null;
  /** The answers the asker names, each with what it implies. It may be empty. */
  options: { label: string; implies: string }[];
  /** The label of the option taken where the person gives none, or null. */
  default: string | null;
  /** The person's answer, an option's label or their own words, or null while the question is open. */
  answer: string | null;
  since: number | null;
}

/** One block or line of a work item's record that the reader did not read, at its 1-based line of `decisions.md`, with the reader's reason. Nothing is decided on it. */
export interface Unread {
  line: number;
  why: string;
}
```

- `WorkItem` gains `decisions: Decision[]` and `questions: Question[]`, each in the order of the file, and `unread: Unread[]`, the reader's refusals and its notices, sorted by line. The field `asked` and the types `Asked` and `AskedQuestion` of phase 3 go.
- `ClientMessage` keeps `{ type: "reverseDecision"; requestId: number; workItemId: number; decisionId: number; message: string }` and `{ type: "answerQuestions"; requestId: number; workItemId: number; answers: { questionId: number; answer: string }[] }`. Each is answered with `done` before the `inbox` that carries the change, or with `error`.
- No server message is added. `ready`, `inbox` and `lastLook` carry the fields with each work item.
- A text the daemon writes is trimmed, cut at 2000 characters as `SessionInfo.stateText` is, and written as one line by the record's writer. A text a stage wrote into the record is sent as it was read.

**`src/daemon/record-times.ts`**, new: the store of `record-times.json`, `{ workItems: Record<number, Record<number, { state: string; since: number }>> }`, the outer key a work item's id and the inner a block's. It is built as `LastLook` is: given no run folder it keeps memory alone, and it writes through `writeWholeFile`. `RecordTimes#since` takes a work item's id, the blocks just read with their states and the clock, holds the change in memory, and returns each block's `since`. `RecordTimes#drop` takes the times of the work items given out of memory. `RecordTimes#flush` writes the file whole where a `since` or a `drop` changed what it holds since the last write, so one pass over every work item writes it once at most. A write that throws leaves the times in memory and is not tried again until the next change.

**`src/daemon/decisions.ts` and `src/daemon/asked.ts` go**, with `decisions.json` and `asked.json`. A daemon of this build neither reads nor writes either file, and leaves one a build of this branch wrote where it stands.

**`src/daemon/daemon.ts`.**

```ts
/** Writes a decision made for a work item in place of asking into the work item's record. Returns it as read back, or undefined where it is refused. */
decided(workItemId: number, decision: { kind: DecisionKind; says: string; grounds: string; by?: string | null }): Decision | undefined;

/** Parks a work item: writes each question into the work item's record as an open block. Returns the questions as read back, or undefined where it is refused. */
park(workItemId: number, asking: { by?: string | null; questions: { asks: string; grounds: string; options?: { label: string; implies: string }[]; default?: string | null }[] }): Question[] | undefined;
```

The daemon holds, for each work item, what it last read of the record, in the protocol's shapes: the decisions, the questions and `unread`. `Daemon#readRecord` reads it and answers whether it reads otherwise than at the last read. `readCommits` calls it for every work item that is not archived before it waits on git for any branch, so a `ready` that goes out before git has answered carries the records, and a work item with no base commit, which `readBranch` returns from at once, has its record read. The archive, the restore, the daemon's start for each archived work item, and each write of the daemon's own call it too. `workItemOf` adds `decisions`, `questions` and `unread` from what was read. The cases `reverseDecision` and `answerQuestions` of `handle` write through `Daemon#personWrote`, which calls `writeRecord`, and a throw of the writer, or of the change it applies, is the request's `error`. `answerQuestions` reads the `open` questions out of the text the write is applied to, so the answers are held against the record as it stands at the write.

`Daemon#readRecord` maps the shapes of #40's module onto the protocol's, and no other module of this work reads a block's field:

- a decision block's `decided` is `Decision.says`, and `Daemon#decided` takes `says` and hands the writer `decided`;
- a decision block's state, an object (`is`, with `message` where it is not `decided`, `commit` where `applied` and `home` where `carried`), is `Decision.state`, the word, `Decision.reversal`, the message or null, and `Decision.landed`, the commit or the home or null;
- a question block's state, `{ is: "open" }` or `{ is: "answered", answer }`, is `Question.answer`, the answer or null;
- `since` on each decision and each question comes from `record-times.json`, keyed by the work item's id and the block's id, and compared by the word of the block's state, since the record holds no clock;
- the record's `refusals` and `notices`, a line and a sentence each, are `WorkItem.unread`, of the type `Unread`, sorted by line.
