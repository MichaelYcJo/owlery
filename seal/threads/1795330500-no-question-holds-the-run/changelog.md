# Changelog: under `agentic`, no question holds the run

Issue #119. A person who handed several work items over and walked away could come back to a run that one question, asked in the middle, had stopped whole. Nothing showed what had been decided for them in place of asking.

- **The policy documents now state the rule, in English and in Korean.** Under the routing answer `agentic`, what needs you is asked once, in one batch, before the first edit. A question that arises later holds only the work item it belongs to, and every other work item goes on. What is decided in place of asking shows on its work item with its grounds, and you can reverse it. The rule is in `docs/product.md` *What we promise* and *Foundation*, in `docs/architecture.md` *Code runs the order, the orchestrator judges*, and in their Korean editions.
- **A work item's line says how many decisions were made for it without asking**, as in "2 decisions without asking". The preview and the work item's view list each one under "Decided without asking", the newest first: whether it was a route, a default taken, a scope chosen or a question the repository already answered, then what was decided, its grounds and its age. A decision holds nothing and raises no notification.
- **You reverse a decision with a message.** In the work item's view, "Reverse" on a decision opens a dialog that takes the reason. The decision stays listed, as reversed, with your message. Owlery keeps the message with the work item. No stage reads it yet: handing a reversal to the next stage comes with the review rounds (#41).
- **A work item held by a question waits for you, alone.** Its line stands under "Waiting for you" and says its first question, followed by "(and 1 more question)" where it has more. The inbox counts it in "waiting for you", and a notification titled with the work item says "Waiting for your answer". Every other work item, and the held one's own terminals and "Ask", go on as before.
- **You answer in the work item's view.** Under "Waiting for your answer" the view shows every question at once, each with its choices, what each choice implies, and a field for another answer. A question's default is already chosen. "Send answers" ends the hold, the section then reads "Answered" and lists each question with your answer, and every control is reached by the keyboard.
- **The summary counts the decisions apart from the work items that wait.** Its sentence reads, for example, "1 waiting for you. 3 decisions made without asking." A work item that gained a decision since you last looked is listed as changed, and its line ends with "2 decisions made without asking." A decision you had already seen is not counted again, reversed or not.
- **Nothing in this release makes a decision or holds a work item yet.** The lines, the view and the summary are in place, and Owlery's background process, the daemon, records both when its own code asks it to. The stages that ask are the review rounds, the gate and the sealer (#41, #42) and the choice of a route (#51). Until one of them is in your build, no work item shows a decision or a question.
- **Decisions and questions outlive the daemon.** They are kept in `decisions.json` and `asked.json` in the daemon's run folder, readable by you alone. Deleting a work item, or removing its repository, removes its entries. Archiving keeps them. Where the daemon's list of repositories was lost and a new work item is given an id an earlier one had, the daemon removes the entries under that id as it gives it, so the new work item never shows another's decisions or questions. The daemon's start removes nothing.
- **After an update, the window shows no decision and no question until the daemon restarts.** A daemon started by the build before this one sends neither, and the status line already names such a daemon and its restart.
- **For a client of the daemon:** a work item carries `decisions` and `asked` in `ready`, in `inbox` and in the last look. Two new messages, `reverseDecision` (with `workItemId`, `decisionId` and `message`) and `answerQuestions` (with `workItemId` and one `answers` entry for each question), are each answered with `done` before the `inbox` that carries the change, or with `error`. No message records a decision or parks a work item.
