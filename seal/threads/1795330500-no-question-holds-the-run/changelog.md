# Changelog: under `agentic`, no question holds the run

Issue #119. A person who handed several work items over and walked away could come back to a run that one question, asked in the middle, had stopped whole. Nothing showed what had been decided for them in place of asking.

- **The policy documents now state the rule, in English and in Korean.** Under the routing answer `agentic`, what needs you is asked once, in one batch, before the first edit. A question that arises later is first decided from the code and the policy, and the decision is recorded as one made without asking. Only what they cannot answer and the session cannot give itself, such as a permission, a credential or an approval, holds the one work item it belongs to, and every other work item goes on. Owlery posts no question on a GitHub issue. The rule is in `docs/product.md` *What we promise* and *Foundation*, in `docs/architecture.md` *Code runs the order, the orchestrator judges* and *What changes because Owlery is not a plugin*, and in their Korean editions.
- **A work item's decisions and questions live in its own records.** Each is a block of `decisions.md` in the work item's directory under `seal/threads/`, in the work item's worktree. Owlery's background process, the daemon, reads that file, committed or not, and keeps no copy. A block written into the file by hand shows in the window within a few seconds. A block the daemon cannot read is not taken for a decision or a question: the preview and the view name its line, as in "Line 16 of decisions.md was not read:", with the reason. The daemon reads `decisions.md` only as a plain file of the worktree, of one mebibyte at most. One that is a link, or that is larger, is not read: the work item shows "Line 1 of decisions.md was not read: the record cannot be read: decisions.md is a link, and Owlery reads it only as a file of its own", or the sentence that gives its size, and a reversal or an answer is refused with the same sentence and leaves the link as it is. A `routing.md` that is a link or larger than that names no work item to the daemon.
- **A work item's line says how many decisions you have not seen and how many reversals wait**, as in "2 decisions without asking, 1 reversed". A decision counts until the next start of the window after you looked. A reversal counts until a stage has taken it in. The preview and the work item's view list every decision under "Decided without asking", the newest first: "Route", "Default taken", "Scope chosen" or "Answered from the tree", then what was decided, its grounds, who decided, and its age. A decision that no longer stands says "Reversed", "Applied in" with the commit, or "Carried to" with where it went, and shows your message. A decision holds nothing and raises no notification.
- **You reverse a decision with a message.** In the work item's view, "Reverse" on a decision opens a dialog that takes the reason. The decision stays listed, as reversed, with your message, which is written into the work item's record. No stage reads it yet: handing a reversal to the next stage comes with the review rounds (#41).
- **A work item held by a question waits for you, alone.** Its line stands under "Needs you" and says its first question, followed by "(and 1 more question)" where it has more. The inbox counts it, as in "1 needs you", and a notification titled with the work item says "Waiting for your answer". Every other work item, and the held one's own terminals and "Ask", go on as before.
- **You answer in the work item's view.** Under "Waiting for your answer" the view shows every question at once, each with who asked, its grounds, its choices, what each choice implies, and a field for another answer. A question's default is already chosen. "Send answers" ends the hold. The section then reads "Answered" and lists each question with your answer, until the next start of the window after you looked. Every control is reached by the keyboard.
- **Owlery commits your reversal and your answers for you.** After either, the daemon commits `decisions.md`, and that file alone, on the work item's branch, with your git identity and the repository's hooks. The subject is "chore: record the person's reversal of decision 1" or "chore: record the person's answers", and the commit ends with the trailer `Seal-Routing:` and the name of the work item's directory. Other work you had staged stays staged, and nothing is pushed. While an agent is working in the work item, in a terminal or in an "Ask", Owlery commits nothing and leaves the file written, so the agent's own commit or the next commit Owlery makes there carries it. Owlery writes and commits your word only on the work item's branch. Where the worktree stands on another branch or on a detached HEAD, or a rebase, a merge, a cherry-pick, a revert or a `git am` is under way in it, Owlery refuses your reversal or your answers before it writes anything: a word written in the middle of a rebase would be lost if the rebase were aborted. The dialog or the section says why, as in "Nothing was written: a rebase is under way in the worktree. Send it again once the worktree is on its branch and nothing is under way in it.", and keeps what you typed, so you send it again afterwards. Where the worktree leaves its branch after your word was written and before its commit, Owlery stages nothing and commits nothing, your word stays written in the file, and the daemon's log says which, as in "the person's word was not committed: the worktree is not on the branch owlery/my-work". Where the commit fails, for example because a hook refuses it, your word stays written in the file, the file is left unstaged, and the daemon's log says what git printed.
- **The summary counts the decisions apart from the work items that need you.** Its sentence reads, for example, "1 needs you. 3 decisions made without asking." A work item that gained a decision since you last looked is listed as changed, and its line ends with "2 decisions made without asking." A decision you had already seen is not counted again, reversed or not.
- **The inbox's filter has the choice "Decided without asking".** It leaves the work items with a decision you can still act on: one that stands, or one you reversed that no stage has taken in.
- **An archive takes a work item's decisions off the inbox and the summary.** They stay listed on its line and in its preview on the archived screen, and in its view again once it is restored. Deleting a work item removes its worktree and the record in it.
- **The stages of "Get a seal" make the decisions and hold a work item.** An orchestrator session decides, from the code and the repository's documents, whether the review rounds are converging, each question a review round or a fix pass raises, and the question of a sealer's `undecided` verdict, and each decision is listed among those made without asking. A question it cannot answer holds the work item, and so does the question of the third `undecided` verdict of one work item and of every later one. The judgment of whether the review rounds are converging is listed as "Route". The route chosen for a work item before its first edit records no decision yet (#51). The first start of "Get a seal" makes the work item's directory.
- **The ages are the daemon's own.** The record holds no time. The daemon keeps when it first read each block in its present state, in `record-times.json` in its run folder, readable by you alone. Where that file is lost, every age starts again and nothing else changes.
- **After an update, the window shows no decision and no question until the daemon restarts.** A daemon started by the build before this one sends neither, and the status line already names such a daemon and its restart.
- **For a client of the daemon:** a work item carries `decisions`, `questions` and `unread` in `ready`, in `inbox` and in the last look. Two new messages, `reverseDecision` (with `workItemId`, `decisionId` and `message`) and `answerQuestions` (with `workItemId` and one `answers` entry for each open question), are each answered with `done` before the `inbox` that carries the change, or with `error`. Either is answered with `error`, and nothing is written, where the work item's worktree is not on its branch or an operation of git is under way in it. The commit is made after the `done`, and its failure changes no answer. No message records a decision or parks a work item.
