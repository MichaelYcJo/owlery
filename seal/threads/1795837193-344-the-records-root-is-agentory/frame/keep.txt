README.md:**IADD, Independent AI-Driven Development, is the method.** Its line is *Zero Self-Approval*: no AI approves its own work. The AIs that frame, craft, proof and inspect the work are independent of one another, and the inspector built none of what it inspects. The inspection is bound to one commit and lapses at the next, so a commit that lands after it shows on the pull request as a lapsed inspection. [docs/iadd.md](docs/iadd.md) is Owlery's definition of the method: its principles, its names, its records and what a workflow is.

apps/desktop/role-bundle/docs/forms/the-round-record.md:**The record's fields stand in `iadd` blocks, and `lib/fields.py` is the one spelling of their grammar.** An `iadd` block is a fenced block opened by a line of three backticks and `iadd`, with nothing before or after them, outside every other fence. The first line of a block names its kind. `target:` opens the round's one block, which stands outside every finding and holds at most one `original:` line. `severity:` opens a finding's one block, which stands in the finding's `### <n>. <symptom>` section with its `location:` and `evidence:` lines and at most one of a `regression:` and a `reopens:` line. `verdict:` opens a finding's close block, at most one in its section, with its `answer:` line and a `location:` line where the fix went to a path the finding does not name. `confirms:` opens a confirmation's block outside every finding. Every line of a block is a field its kind takes, written `<key>: <value>` with the key exactly as the module spells it and the value matching the key's shape whole; a line that is not, a key over its count and a blank line are refused at their line, and the block is not read past it. Nothing outside a block is a field: a line shaped like one in prose decides nothing and is named by nothing, and where a field is owed its absence is refused. A finding with no close block is open. The record holds one `## Findings` heading, under which each `### <n>. <symptom>` heading opens a finding, and no two findings share a number. No `Record language` translates a key, a value's fixed words or the info string.
