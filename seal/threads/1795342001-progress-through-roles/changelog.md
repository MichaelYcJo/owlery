# Changelog: a work item's view and its preview show how far it has come through the roles

Issue #46. A work item's view showed its agent's words, its commits and its changed files, and nothing said how far the work item had come through framer, crafter, proofer and sealer. While its review rounds ran, its line of the inbox said "Working" with no stage and no age.

- **A work item's view and its preview in the inbox draw an approval line.** The section "Approval line" stands after what the agent says. It holds four places in the order Framer, Crafter, Proofer, Sealer, each with a mark, the role's name and a note of two words at most. A place that has not started reads "Not started", with the mark of a work item asleep. Night watch and Desk draw the places in a row joined by rules, and Register draws four ruled boxes. The sealer's place also holds the seal's place.
- **The work item's records say how each place ended.** The framer's place reads "Framed" where the work item's directory holds a design. The crafter's reads the commits the branch holds after its base, for example "3 commits". The proofer's reads the review rounds recorded, for example "2 rounds". The sealer's reads the last verdict: "Sealed", "Withheld" with the mark of a failure, or "Undecided" with the mark of a wait, and "Gate passed" where the tests' broad run is recorded and no verdict stands. Owlery reads the records in the work item's worktree, committed or not, every few seconds while the window is open.
- **A place says which stage runs for its role, and that a stage failed.** While the review rounds run, the proofer's place reads, for example, "Round 2" with the mark of work, and the crafter's reads "Fix pass" during a fix pass. A stage that failed or that you stopped marks its role's place "Failed", and the reason stands under the places as a sentence, for example "Proofer: The session was stopped at a request before it finished." The orchestrator's stages mark no place.
- **A place waits where its role asked you a question.** A question the proofer or the crafter raised, and that parks the work item, marks that role's place "Needs you", with the question under the places.
- **Each mark says its place's whole words to a pointer and to a screen reader**, for example "Proofer: Round 2, 4 min", "Crafter: Fix pass on round 1 failed" or "Sealer: Sealed at 1a2b3c4".
- **A work item's line of the inbox names the stage that runs.** While the rounds run a stage, the line's second row reads, for example, "Proofer, round 2", "Crafter, fix pass" or "Orchestrator, judgment", and the line's age counts from the stage's start, in the inbox, the sidebar and the summary. A line that waits for you keeps saying what it waits on: a question, a call to allow, or its agent's own question.
- **The line does not say whether a verdict still holds, and opens no stage's conversation.** The sealer's place does not say whether a verdict still holds for the branch's newest commit: the mark's words name the commit the verdict was given at. The seal's place stays unlit.
- **After an update, the view shows no approval line until the daemon restarts.** A daemon started by the build before this one sends no record of the roles, and the status line already names a daemon of another build.
- **For a client of the daemon:** wherever the daemon sends a work item, the work item carries `recorded`: `framed`, `rounds`, `gate` (the commit of the last broad run recorded, or `null`) and `verdict` (`is`, one of `sealed`, `withheld` and `undecided`, with `commit` and `says`, or `null`). The last look holds no `recorded`. A client reads its absence as a work item with no approval line. No message is added.
