## Scope

In:

1. **A screen sends a returned pane nothing until its replay is drawn.** A pane that this window drew before since the page loaded, and that takes a screen again, is sent nothing from the moment it takes the screen until the terminal has drawn the replay the window writes into that screen for that turn. That covers what a person types, what a person pastes, and what the terminal answers. `apps/desktop/src/renderer/view.ts#screenTurns` holds the rule, and `apps/desktop/src/renderer/work-item-view.ts#take` tells it whether the window drew the pane before. A pane the window draws for the first time since the page loaded is sent what it is sent today.
2. **Unit cases pin item 1** in the `screenTurns` block of `apps/desktop/test/view.test.ts`. A key typed after the terminal reached the returned pane's turn and before its replay is handed goes to nobody, and a key typed once the replay is drawn goes to the pane. A returned pane that leaves the screen before its replay came leaves no silence behind: the next pane, drawn for the first time, receives keys as a first pane does today. The existing cases of the block stay as they are.
3. **The comments say the rule.** The comments of `screenTurns`, of `ScreenTurns` and of `work-item-view.ts#write` are rewritten in place to state item 1.
4. **`pasteOnceBack` waits by time.** It presses "Paste" again while the pane's process was handed nothing, until 15 seconds have passed, the bound `BuiltApp.until` uses. Its comment states the rule as item 1 makes it true.
5. **The menu-open case waits for the replay before it reads the screen.** After the click on the second pane's tab at line 995, and before "Select All" and "Copy" read what the pane printed, the case waits until the link on the pane's first row is drawn again.
6. **A case of the file that fails leaves the next case one tab and "Side by side" off.** After a case that failed, every pane `openPane` opened since the file started and that the daemon still holds is closed, and "Side by side" is off again. After a case that passed, the step does nothing. A case that passes leaves what the case after it reads: it closes its panes itself, or it hands its pane on. The case "makes a path a pane printed a link…" hands its pane to the case "shows a path's file in Finder…", which reads it and closes it, so a step that closed panes after every case made the Finder case fail (*Grounding*, the rows of the build). The step closes every pane `openPane` opened and not those of the failed case alone, since the failed case may hold a pane an earlier case handed it.
7. **The version moves to 0.0.4.** Line 3 of `apps/desktop/package.json` names it, and so do lines 33, 36 and 38 of `.github/workflows/owlery-seal.yml`. A case of `apps/desktop/test/records-place.test.ts` holds the workflow file to the version (*Grounding*), so one commit changes both files.
8. **The changelog fragment.** The work item's `changelog.md` says what item 1 changes for a person, and its last bullet says "The desktop app's version is 0.0.4."
9. **Four more cases wait for the replay before they send.** The rule of item 1 drops a key that the Command-key case types before the first pane's replay is drawn, where the app sent it before. The Shift+Enter case has the same shape. The first drop case and the two-drop case drop files before that replay is drawn, and the drop arrives without brackets or not at all, as it could before this work. *Grounding*, the rows of the build, names the four and how the list was made. Each of the four waits until the link on the first pane's first row is drawn again, before its first key or its drop: the wait of item 5. One helper of the file holds that wait, and the menu-open case calls it in place of its two lines. The helper is told which tile it reads. In the two-drop case that is the tile the drop lands on, the one without the keyboard. The four are built here and not left to a later issue because they share one shape and one wait with the menu-open case, and the Command-key case's race is one this work's rule made.

Out:

- **A pane's first replay in a window.** A pane the window draws for the first time since the page loaded, a pane a person just opened among them, still receives keys before its replay arrives and while it is drawn. The terminal's answers to that replay's questions must reach the pane's program, and they leave by the same path as a person's keys, so the screen cannot hold one back and send the other. `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered* holds the wider rule and why it was not chosen. The cases of item 9 wait for the replay whether it is a first one or a later one. That is a wait in the case, and it leaves this rule of the app as it is.
- **The cases of the file other than the two of the issue and the four of item 9.** They keep their waits for "one tab": item 6 gives them the base they start from after a failed case. None of them asserts that bytes reach a pane that just came back (*Grounding*, the rows of the build). The three cases that assert a drop or a drag types nothing stay as they are.
- **The rows the settle of release 0.0.2 carried into issue #168.** Each names a mechanism other than this one: the debugging port's round trips at the runner's first start, a click lost when the sidebar is drawn between its press and its release, a case of `apps/desktop/test/daemon.test.ts` that counts inboxes, and the bounds of cases under a heavier load. None of them is built here. `frame/questions.md` rows Q2 and Q3 hold where they go.
- **The count of runs on the hosted runner.** The issue's third *Done when* line is counted after the pull request opens. `frame/questions.md` row Q1 holds it.
- **The daemon.** No message of the protocol changes, and the daemon answers an `attach` as it does today.

## Data & interfaces

- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/view.ts`: `ScreenTurns.take` takes, beside the pane, whether the window drew the pane before. `receiver()` answers with nothing while the screen's turn belongs to such a pane and the terminal has not drawn the replay written in that turn. The silence belongs to the turn: `giveBack` and the next `take` end it.
- `apps/desktop/src/renderer/work-item-view.ts#take` hands `screenTurns` what its note of drawn panes, `replayed`, says of the pane at that moment. `write` hands a later replay to the screen as it does today.
- No message of `apps/desktop/src/shared/protocol.ts` changes. No environment variable, no setting and no file of the run folder is added.
- `apps/desktop/test/terminal-basics.test.ts`: `openPane` notes each pane it opened. A step that runs after each case acts where the case failed: it closes the noted panes the daemon still holds and puts "Side by side" off. `closeFromElsewhere` takes the panes asked for, closes those among them that the daemon's `ready` lists, and answers with the panes it closed.
- The same file: one helper waits until the link on the first row of a tile is drawn again, and then takes the pointer off the link. It takes the tile it reads, and reads the pane in front where none is named. The pointer helpers it stands on, `cell` among them, read the first tile that is not hidden today, so they come to take the tile as well. The helper presses nothing and gives no pane the keyboard, so the first drop case can still assert that the pane has no keyboard before the drop.
