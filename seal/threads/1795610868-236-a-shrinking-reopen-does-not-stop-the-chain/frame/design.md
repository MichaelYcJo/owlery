## Scope

In:

1. **The whole read binds whoever makes a fix pass.** `apps/desktop/role-bundle/skills/implement/fix-pass.md` *The narrow set, then the read of the range* says that whoever makes the pass makes the reading `agents/crafter.md` *Before you hand back, your range and the work item's records are read whole against HEAD* asks for: a crafter, and a pane's session that makes the pass itself (`--by the session`). For the session, the text says what the crafter's *Job* and hand-back stand for: the session corrects each statement HEAD contradicts in its range and in the work item's records, since in a pane it built the work and wrote those records, and names in the close block's `answer:` each one it leaves.
2. **A fix pass corrects a fact at every place that states it.** `apps/desktop/role-bundle/agents/crafter.md` *Before you hand back, ...* says that in a fix pass, a finding that corrects a fact, a statement of which things hold something, is corrected at every place that states it. The places are found two ways: the whole read of the work item's records, which is a closed set, and `git grep` on the fact's words across the tree, which is a sample and is called one.
3. **The close block says it did.** `fix-pass.md` *The close blocks, last* says that for such a finding the close block's `answer:` names the search the pass ran and the places it corrected beyond the finding's `location:` lines, or says that it found none.
4. **A finding in the records alone is no signal.** `apps/desktop/role-bundle/agents/orchestrator.md` *The rounds are converging where each round leaves less to do than the round before it* gains a paragraph below the list. A finding is located in the records where every path its `location:` lines name stands in the work item's directory. Such a finding counts toward none of the first four signals: not by its `reopens:` or `regression:` line, not in the count of open findings of the last round or of the round before it, which the third signal compares, and not by its location. So the third signal compares the two rounds' findings located outside the records, and holds where those of the last round are no fewer and no lighter than those of the round before it (round-1#1). The signal of a finding closed `rejected` and raised again holds for every finding. The paragraph gives the grounds in one or two sentences: a finding in the records is a correction of text that the fix pass's whole read ends (*Scope* items 1 to 3 and 8), and where the daemon runs the chain, the round ceiling bounds it.
5. **A `no` argues for its stop.** The same file's paragraph opening **A `yes` starts the fix pass, and a `no` stops the chain for the person.** loses "so a `no` costs one stop and no work", which the issue shows false: the stop costs the person's attention, and while nobody watches it costs the hours until somebody does. In its place, the paragraph says what a `no` buys: the person's decision at the stop, which no fix pass makes, such as a reframe, a split, dropping the work or a fix by hand, and the rounds a diverging chain would spend before anything else stops it. It cites `skills/core/SKILL.md` *Asking a person is the most expensive step*, and says that the `grounds:` line names the signal and the findings that hold it. So a records-only reopen, where the person's restart would only run the same fix pass, buys no decision, and the paragraph of *Scope* item 4 counts it toward no signal.
6. **Cases pin the texts.** `apps/desktop/test/role-bundle.test.ts` pins the sentences of *Scope* items 1 to 5 and 8. Its existing orchestrator case keeps its pins. Each new pin is seen red with only its sentence deleted (the bundle's contract rule 15).
7. **The changelog fragment**, `changelog.md` of this work item, in the last phase, and in a fix pass whose change it states.
8. **A crafter's fix pass corrects the frame's lines that state the fact a finding corrects (round-1#2).** The owl chain runs no framer, and in the daemon's chain no party reads a fix pass's answer beyond its questions (*Grounding*, the row on the crafter's *Job*). So a frame line a crafter names for a fold is never folded, and the grounds of *Scope* item 4, that the fix pass corrects the fact at every place that states it, fail for a crafter's pass. Two units of `apps/desktop/role-bundle/agents/crafter.md` change:
   - *Job*: in a fix pass, the crafter's writes take in each line of the work item's records that states the fact a finding corrects, a line of the frame among them. The crafter rewrites such a line to what HEAD gives, or removes it, and writes no new account of what was built. Every other line of the frame stays outside its writes, as before.
   - *Before you hand back, your range and the work item's records are read whole against HEAD*: after the sentence that ends "which is a sample and is called one.", the rule says that in a fix pass a place in a line of the frame is corrected too, and that in a fix pass each item the rule has the crafter name in its hand-back goes instead into the `answer:` of the close block of the finding it concerns, and otherwise into the `answer:` of the last close block, since no party of the owl chain folds a fix pass's hand-back. That is the form `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` already gives a pane's session (*Scope* item 1).

   Its inputs are the open findings of the round record the pass closes and the files the whole read covers: `routing.md`, the three frame files, `gaps.md` and `changelog.md`. It applies wherever a crafter makes a fix pass, listed from what launches one: a role session of the daemon at the stage `fix-pass` (`apps/desktop/src/daemon/adapters/claude-roles.ts`, "crafter at fix-pass") and a crafter subagent a pane's session starts for `fix-pass` (`skills/seal/SKILL.md` *The loop*). Both read `agents/crafter.md`. A pane's session that makes the pass itself already corrects the frame (*Scope* item 1).

## What the daemon could do beyond the issue

Each item is the daemon reading an exact fact, never deciding the meaning. None is in this work's scope. The orchestrator takes what is cheap and in scope and files issues for the rest.

1. **Hand the judgment the facts it reads.** `convergencePrompt` could list, for each open finding of the last round, whether every path its `location:` lines name stands in the work item's directory, and each `reopens:` and `regression:` line. That is a path prefix and a line read, so the orchestrator would not have to read the locations itself. #235 rewrites `convergencePrompt` in this release, so this waits for #235's merge. Cost: one function and its case. An issue.
2. **Skip the orchestrator's session where the rule answers by itself.** `gateBefore` could return `none` where every open finding of the last round is located in the records and none was closed `rejected` and raised again, which saves one role session per such round. It moves part of a judgment of meaning into code, which `docs/roadmap.md` *Releases and the tracker* keeps in the bundle, so it needs the owner's word. An issue, for after the walk (#184) shows how the model judges.
3. **A round ceiling in a pane.** The chain command's `owed` could stop at `ROUNDS_CEILING` as the daemon's chain does. Today a pane is bounded by the session's judgment and the person alone, and *Scope* item 4 widens what that judgment lets through. Cost: `owed` and its cases, in `chain-command.ts`, which #223 also changes. An issue (Q2).
4. **Measure what a stop bought.** The settle's `--list` already prints the decisions made without asking. It could count each `no` after which the person's next start ran the fix pass with no reversal, which is a stop that bought nothing. That is the record `skills/core/SKILL.md` *The rule a mechanism is kept against* asks for before the judgment is kept or dropped (`plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row that removes the judgment). An issue.
5. **Name a fix commit that touched code after a records-only finding.** The daemon already refuses a `fixed` commit that changed no path the finding's `location:` lines name (`chain.test.ts:1791`). It could report, and block nothing, a fix of a finding located in the records whose commit changed a path outside them. Cheap, and of little worth until a run shows the case. No issue now.
6. **Keep what a role hands back beyond its questions.** `stage-records.ts#recordFixPass` reads a fix pass's answer for its questions alone, so whatever else a crafter hands back reaches no record (round-1#2). *Scope* item 8 moves a fix pass's items into its close blocks. The daemon could also keep the rest of each role's answer with the stage it stored, where the window shows it. Cost: the stage's stored text and its view. An issue, for the orchestrator to weigh.
