# Changelog: a finding in the work item's records alone stops no owl chain, and whoever makes a fix pass reads the records whole

Issue #236. In #225's run, a pane's session made the fix passes itself. Rounds 2 and 3 each reopened the finding of the round before, and every one of those findings stood in the work item's records alone. The convergence judgment stopped the chain for the person before round 3's fix pass, though the person's restart could only run the same fix pass. The role bundle also said that a `no` "costs one stop and no work", and it bound only the crafter to read the work item's records whole in a fix pass.

- **Whoever makes a fix pass reads its range and the work item's records whole.** `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` *The narrow set, then the read of the range* binds the crafter and a pane's session that makes the pass itself and records it with `--by the session`. For a pane's session, the crafter's *Job* stands for the work item, so the session corrects every statement HEAD contradicts in its range and in the work item's records. The crafter's hand-back stands for the close blocks, so the session names what it leaves in an `answer:` line.
- **A fix pass corrects a fact at every place that states it.** `agents/crafter.md` *Before you hand back, your range and the work item's records are read whole against HEAD* says that a finding that corrects a fact is corrected at every place that states the fact, and not at its `location:` lines alone. The places are found by the whole read of the work item's records and by `git grep` on the fact's words across the tree, which is a sample and is called one. In a fix pass, the crafter's *Job* gives it each line of the three frame files that states the fact a finding corrects, to rewrite to what HEAD gives or to remove. What its hand-back would name goes into a close block's `answer:` instead, since no party of the owl chain folds a fix pass's hand-back.
- **The close block says how the places were found.** `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` *The close blocks, last* has the `answer:` of such a finding name the search the pass ran and each place beyond the finding's `location:` lines that the pass corrected, or say that the search found none.
- **A finding located in the work item's records alone counts toward none of the first four convergence signals.** `agents/orchestrator.md` *The rounds are converging where each round leaves less to do than the round before it* says that a finding is located in the records where every path its `location:` lines name stands in the work item's directory. Such a finding holds no signal by its `reopens:` or `regression:` line, by the counts of open findings that the last round and the round before it are compared on, or by its location two rounds in a row. So the third signal compares the two rounds' findings outside the records, and a last round whose findings outside the records are no fewer and no lighter than those of the round before still says the rounds are not converging. Where the last round has no open finding outside the records, the third signal does not hold. A finding closed `rejected` and raised again still says so too, wherever it stands. On records shaped like #225's run, the rule answers `converging: yes` after rounds 2 and 3, before the fix pass on each one's record.
- **A `no` argues for the stop it costs.** The same file no longer says that a `no` costs one stop and no work. It says that a stop is the most expensive step of the chain, what a `no` buys (the person's decision at the stop, such as a reframe, a split, dropping the work or a fix by hand, and the rounds a chain that does not converge would spend), and that its `grounds:` line names the signal that holds and the findings that hold it.
- **The daemon's code does not change.** The daemon still asks for the judgment before the fix pass on round 2 or a later round that left a finding open, a `no` still stops the chain, and the ceiling of rounds still stops it with no judgment.
- **What no run has shown yet.** No model was run on the new text. Whether the orchestrator and a pane's session judge `yes` on records like #225's and still `no` on a code fix that did not hold is the owner's walk's to show (#184).
- **AgenticSeal has the same gap.** Its `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` also binds only the crafter to the whole read, and MichaelYcJo/AgenticSeal#1623 asks for the same fix there.
