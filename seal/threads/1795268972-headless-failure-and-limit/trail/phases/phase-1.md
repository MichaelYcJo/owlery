**Decisions made while building, each with its grounds.**

- `headlessDropped` is sent only when the bound takes a session off the list. A delete or a repository's removal still takes its work items' sessions off the list and sends nothing for them. Judged against `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 7, which names the bound alone. Its *Data & interfaces* says the message is sent "when a session leaves the list", which reads wider. The two sentences disagree, and the narrower one was built. The protocol's comment on `headlessDropped` says what was built. The orchestrator folds the frame.
- The answer `done` to `stopHeadless` goes out before the `headless` message that carries the failed session. Judged against the protocol's own note on `done` ("before the `inbox` that carries what it changed") and the archive's "the answer first".
- A run whose adapter is still starting is not waited for at the daemon's close, nor in `endSessions`'s wait. It is stopped once it has started, as before. Judged against `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 6, "the stop of every run it stops", and against two existing cases. "stops a run that starts only after the daemon closed" expects no stop when `close` returns. "stops the run of a headless session a removal ended while its adapter was still starting" expects none at the removal's `done`. Waiting for the start could also take the login shell's lookup, up to 10 seconds, past the app's 5-second wait (`docs/architecture.md` *Overall layout*).
- `RoleRun.stop()` settles at the process's `exit` event. The listener's `exit` event can come up to 300 ms later, at the streams' `close` or at the grace. Judged against `frame/design.md` *Data & interfaces*: "settled once the process has exited".
- The grace after the exit is `STREAMS_GRACE_MS` in `claude.ts`, not exported. The frame gives its 300 ms and names no constant for it.
- `HEADLESS_LIMIT_MS` and `HEADLESS_KEPT` are exported from `daemon.ts`, so that a case pins each value. This departs from `frame/design.md` *Data & interfaces*, which names them as constants and exports only the adapter's two. The rule changes give 60 minutes and 50 with `check: apps/desktop/test/daemon.test.ts`. Before the export, no case held either number.
- The limit's timer has no guard of its own. `headlessChanged` clears it at the change that takes a session out of `working`, and `close` clears it. Every change that takes a session out of `working` goes through `headlessChanged`: `failed` in `startHeadless`, the result, and the new `stopHeadless` method that the limit, the request and `endSessions` share. Read. So one mechanism holds the rule "the timer ends when the session ends", and mutants 7 and 8 of `daemon.ts` show it is watched.
- `test/view.test.ts`'s `headless` helper gains `errorOutput: ""` and `stopped: null`, so that the tree typechecks with the new required fields. It is the one line phase 1 changes in phase 2's test file.

**For phase 2.** The window has no case for `headlessDropped` in `renderer.ts`'s message switch, so it ignores the message for now. `src/renderer/shared.ts:24` says the window's headless list is "each replaced whole at every `headless` since", and it will also lose a session at `headlessDropped`. A window's stop is `{ type: "stopHeadless", requestId, id }`, answered with `done` before the `headless` message that carries the failed session, and with `error` `no headless session <id>` for an id the daemon does not list. A failed session's `errorOutput` is `""` until its process has exited, and it can arrive in a second `headless` message after the one that carries `failed`.
