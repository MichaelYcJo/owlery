# Changelog: the built app's timed cases hold without a retry

Issue #117. Three cases of the desktop suite that start the built app failed on some runs. Two failed on GitHub's macOS runner: the Open in submenu's pointer case and the case that opens a work item from its worktree's link. The third, the link card case, failed on the owner's Mac and never on the runner. A person's pointer passing over the test's window can take its card away: one mouse move handed to the app's process did so. Nothing else this work tried did, neither the page's processor slowed twentyfold nor load averages of 52 to 91. No failing run was caught with a pointer moving, so whether a pointer did so in the runs that failed is the owner's to say. The Open in case raced its 300 ms pause against the round trips through Electron's debugging port, and it passed with the page slowed eightfold, so a slow page was not its cause either.

- **A window started with `OWLERY_BACKGROUND=1` lets your pointer through.** Such a start is a test's or an agent's. Its window stood in the middle of your screen and took your mouse: a click of yours landed in it, and a move of yours over it reached its page and took a test's pointer off a link. The window now ignores the system's mouse, so your pointer reaches the windows under it. The test or agent drives the window through Electron's debugging port, as before. `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/roadmap.md` say so where they describe such a start.
- **The submenu's pointer case no longer races its 300 ms pause.** Each move through the debugging port waits on the page's answer, and on the runner the move back came after the pause ran out. The pointer's way from the submenu across the menu's next item and back reaches the page in one of its tasks. The case still fails where the pause is removed, or shortened where its timer starts or where its length is named.
- **The cases that point at a pane's link wait until the pane's text is drawn.** The terminal looks for the links of a row once, as the pointer comes onto it. A pane's output, or the replay of a pane whose view has just come in front, could arrive after the case's pointer. The pointer now comes again until the link shows.
- **The link card case times the card's pauses by the page's clock.** It no longer reads the card in the moment before a pause runs out, and it fails where either pause is shortened, where its timer starts or where its length is named.
- **The runner runs each case once.** While these causes were open, #30 had `apps/desktop/vitest.config.ts` run a failed case a second time where `CI` is set. This work removes that retry, so a case that fails once on the runner fails the run. This release never ships the retry, so #30's fragment no longer names it.
