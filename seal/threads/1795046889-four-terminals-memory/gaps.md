## Not done

Three launches of each scenario were not made in phase 1. The spawn asked for as few starts of the app as the phase needs on a machine its owner works at, and the phase's comparison, eight terminals against one, does not need them.

`korean-pane.ts#openReceiverPane` and `memory.ts#openWorkItem` both fill the "New work item" form, and no shared function was made of them. It would change the Korean case's file, which this phase's plan row does not name, and the two differ in what they wait for: the receiver's file, or the tab and its terminal.

The measurement prints the date and the versions of macOS, Electron and xterm.js. It does not print the commit, the machine and its displays, the window's size or a tile's size. `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 7 asks them of `apps/desktop/measurements/memory.md`, which phase 4 writes.

A launch that is thrown away is not made again by the tool. The run fails, and whoever ran it runs it again.

The cut at a line's start reads at most the first 4,096 characters kept for a line break, on each append that drops output (`apps/desktop/src/daemon/buffer.ts#dropLineRest`, the bound of `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 5). Phase 2 measured 29 microseconds an append for pieces of 64 characters where the search read all 256K characters kept. The bounded search was not timed: it reads at most 4,096 characters whatever is kept.

`eight-in-turn` after the pool was launched once in phase 3, not three times, for the reason phase 1's three scenarios were: the fall from eight terminals to one shows on one launch.

Nothing answers a question a program asks its terminal while its pane is off the stage. The pane holds no screen then, and when it comes back the answers to its replay are sent only where that replay is the first the window draws of it since the window loaded (`apps/desktop/src/renderer/work-item-view.ts#write`). Before this work a hidden pane kept its terminal, which answered at once. The other two choices were weighed and not taken: sending every replay's answers types each old answer into the pane again at every press of its tab, which phase 3's first drive read, and sending none leaves a pane's first questions unanswered where it printed them before the window asked for its output. A screen state the daemon keeps is what would answer them, and `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 9 puts it out of scope.

While the terminal draws a later replay, what a person types into that screen is not sent, because xterm.js hands the window a person's keys and the terminal's answers the same way. The span was not timed.

The window does not hold back the size of an exited pane's screen, though it knows the pane's process ended. The daemon of this branch takes that size and changes nothing (`38dce05`), and only a daemon left running by an earlier build fails on it. Between the moment a pane's process ends and the moment the daemon learns of it, a size sent for that pane could still reach the closed pseudo-terminal; nothing catches that, as nothing did before the work.

`paneOnScreen` answers the pane in front alone. With one pane on the stage that is every pane on it, and phase 4 widens it with `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 1.

A launch fails where `footprint` gave no number for any process of the app (`apps/desktop/test/memory.ts#unread`, round 1's finding 1), and no process of the app is let off. A helper that Chromium starts and ends between the read of `ps` and the read of `footprint` would fail a launch that way. No launch has been made since the rule, so nobody has seen whether one does. The by-kind rows of phase 1's three kept launches hold the same five kinds (`trail/phases/phase-1.md`).
