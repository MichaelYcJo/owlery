# Questions for the planner: the Open in submenu (#109)

The issue left these judgments open, and the tree answered each. Its grounds stand where the line points.

- Whether the system draws the submenu or the window does: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on the system's menu.
- Whether `menu.ts` is kept or replaced: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the first two rows.
- What an item of the submenu is called: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on the labels.
- What the menu holds where no app is installed: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on "Open in Finder" as an item of its own.
- How the pointer opens the submenu and when it closes: `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 4, and `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on the pause.
- Which keys move into the submenu and out of it: `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 5.
- Where the submenu stands near the window's edge: `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 7, and `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on `menu.ts`.
- How a case presses an item of the submenu without opening an app: `frame/design.md` *Grounding*, the lines on `openFolder` and on the daemon, and scenario 9.

One assumption is written down and not asked, because either answer builds the same code: scenario 10's case may bring a menu to the window's edge by a press near the right edge of a line or by a narrower page, and the builder writes which in the phase record.

No row is open. How the submenu looks when drawn, in the two themes, is no question of the frame: no case reads it, and the builder names it in `gaps.md` for the owner's hand check.
