# Changelog: the inbox-first window

Issue #18. The app opened on a pane of terminals with the inbox as a narrow list beside it, so its first impression was a terminal with many buttons. A person who came back could not see what had changed while they were away.

- **The app opens on the inbox, and starts no terminal.** No shell opens in your home folder at start any more.
- **A line of the inbox is two rows.** The first holds an icon for the agent's state, the work item's title and how long the state has lasted. The second says what the agent asks or last said. Where the agent said nothing it is the newest commit's subject, and where there is no commit, the branch. A badge such as "2 new" counts the commits the branch gained since you last looked.
- **The lines stand in groups, in the order you act on them:** "Waiting for you", "Working", "Finished" and "No agent", each with its count. A sentence under the word "Inbox" counts the states, as in "1 waiting for you, 2 working".
- **A sidebar stands at the left of every screen.** It holds a search field, "Inbox", "Summary" and "History", and each repository that has a work item with its work items under it. A bell counts the work items that wait for you. A "+" opens the "New work item" form, and so does N with the Command key, or with the Control key where the keyboard has no Command key.
- **A search and a filter narrow the sidebar and the list together.** K with the Command key, or with the Control key where the keyboard has none, puts the keyboard in the search field. A work item matches by its title, its branch or its repository's name, and the letters that match are marked. The filter shows the work items of one state.
- **A preview beside the list shows the selected work item:** its state and how long it has lasted, its title, its branch, how many commits it is ahead of the branch it was cut from, its repository, what the agent asks or said, its commits by hash and subject with their added and removed lines, and its changed files. A dot marks each commit that is new since you last looked. J and K move the selection, and Enter opens the work item. The keys are read by their place on the keyboard, so they act under a Korean input source.
- **A work item opens in a view of its own.** Its terminals are in the middle. A column at the right holds its facts, what the agent asks or said, its commits, its changed files, "Ask" and, at the foot, the seal's place. Opening a view starts nothing.
- **Terminals live inside a work item.** The buttons that open a pane are in the work item's view, one per kind of pane. The window offers no pane outside a work item.
- **"Ask" asks in the work item you are looking at,** whether or not it has a pane, and lists that work item's sessions alone.
- **When you come back, a summary comes first.** Where a work item changed since you last looked, the app opens on one screen that says it in one sentence, as in "1 waiting for you", with a line per changed work item: "Waits for you. 2 new commits since you were last here." It holds no terminal. Where a changed work item waits, Enter or "Answer the first question" opens it, and "Open the inbox" goes on to the inbox. There the titles of the work items that changed are drawn stronger and heavier.
- **The summary is skipped where nothing changed,** and on the first run. The sidebar's "Summary" opens it at any time, with the changes found when the app was opened.
- **"History" lists the work items whose agent finished,** newest first. The inbox keeps them too.
- **A look is recorded when the window loses focus, is hidden or closes.** The daemon keeps one, in `last-look.json` in its run folder, so it outlasts the app and the daemon.
- **New commits made while the app was closed count.** The daemon reads each work item's new commits from git before it answers a window that connects. It waits 500 milliseconds at most, and where git takes longer the counts follow a moment later.
- **The window has two themes, Night desk and Letter.** Night desk is dark and is the theme of a first run. Letter is paper, with titles in a serif typeface. A control at the sidebar's foot chooses "Night desk", "Letter" or "Match the system", which follows the system's dark or light appearance while the window is open. The app keeps the choice in `appearance.json` in its user data folder and opens in it the next time. A theme changes colours, typefaces, texture and corners, and never the layout or the words.
- **Red stands on the seal alone.** A button, a mark, the ring of the keyboard's focus and a failure take another colour in both themes.
- **The window opens at 1440 by 900 with its title bar hidden,** so on macOS the traffic lights stand inside the sidebar. It draws in the system's typefaces and fetches nothing from the network.
- **A seal's place is on every line, in the preview and in a view, and it is blank.** On a line it is a blank disc. In the preview and at the foot of a view it is AgenticSeal's owl seal pressed into the ground with no wax on it. Each is named "No seal yet". No work item can be sealed before milestone 2.
- **The list of hook events is gone from the window.** Set `OWLERY_DEBUG_HOOKS=1` to have the daemon write each event's name to `daemon.log`.
- **The owl emblem is not under the Apache License.** `TRADEMARKS.md`, linked from both READMEs, says that the Owlery and AgenticSeal names, the emblem and its two drawings in the tree are reserved.
- **Not yet:** a summary of what changed after the app was opened, a reply to the agent from the window, the choices an agent offers with a question, how far a branch is behind the branch it was cut from, and anything that needs a seal.
- **For a client of the daemon:** `looked`, with no field, records a look and is answered with nothing; `ready` carries `lastLook`, or `null` where no client ever looked; a hello is answered after the counts are read, and what a client sends before its `ready` is handled after it. A session carries `stateSince`, `stateText` and `stateTool`. A work item carries `repoName`, `baseBranch`, `commits` and `changes`.
