## Scope

1. **Three screens in the one window, one shown at a time**: the summary screen of item 19, the inbox screen and a work item's view. At every load, the reload after the daemon went away among them, the window shows the summary screen where item 19 says it opens, and the inbox screen otherwise. Which screen is in front is the window's own state. No screen is shown before the daemon's `ready` arrives. The bar of item 2 is one element outside the three screens, shown above the summary screen and the inbox screen alike.
2. **The inbox screen** takes the window's full width. A bar at its top holds the emblem and the name of item 15 and the control that opens the "New work item" form. Under the bar a list column at the left, 620 pixels wide, holds the sentence of item 3 and one line per work item, and the preview of item 17 takes the rest of the width. An inbox with no work item says so and keeps the control. No terminal is displayed on the inbox screen.
   - The lines stand in groups, each under its heading, in this order: "Waiting for you", "Working", "Finished", and "No agent" for a work item whose line has no state. A group no work item is in is left out, heading and all. Inside a group the lines keep the order the daemon lists them in.
   - A line holds five parts: the title, the branch, the agent's state, the new commits, and the seal's place of item 15, which is the line's last part. The first four are what `inboxLine` of `src/renderer/view.ts` gives today, unchanged. The group's heading says the state in words, and a dot at the head of the line says it in the state's colour.
   - A press on a line opens its work item's view.
3. **The inbox's sentence** (`inboxSummary`) says how many work items are in each agent state, the state a person must act on first: `waiting`, then `working`, then `finished`, as in "1 waiting for you, 2 working". A state no work item is in is left out, and the sentence is empty where no line has a state. It is not the summary screen of item 19, which speaks of the work items that changed alone.
4. **A work item's view** opens when its line is pressed, or as item 17 and item 19 say. It is three columns. Opening a view starts no process.

Where the work item has no tab, the area says so and offers the same buttons.

18. **The daemon keeps when a person last looked.**
    - A client sends `looked`, a message with no field. The daemon then keeps one record, the look: the time, and the lists of sessions and of work items as an `inbox` sent at that moment would carry them. A later `looked` replaces the record. The daemon writes the record into its run folder as it writes the list of work items, so a daemon started again on that folder holds it.
    - The daemon answers a `looked` with nothing and sends nothing to any client for it. A client learns of a look in its next `ready` alone.
    - `ready` gains `lastLook`, the record as it stood when the client said hello, or `null` where no client ever sent `looked`. The window reads a `ready` without the field, which a daemon of an earlier build sends, as `null`.
    - **A `ready` carries the counts of new commits as git gives them at that hello.** The daemon reads every work item's count when a client says hello and sends `ready` when the read ends, so the first `ready` of a daemon started again carries counts and no `null` for a work item git can count, and a `ready` sent after the daemon had no client carries the commits that landed meanwhile. The daemon waits 500 milliseconds for the read at most, under the second the app gives a daemon to answer (`## Grounding`, the bullet on `daemon-client.ts`); where the read has not ended by then, `ready` carries the counts the daemon holds, and the read's result follows in an `inbox` as today. A message the client sends after its hello is handled after its `ready` is sent. A count the read changes goes to the other clients in an `inbox`, as today. One read of the counts runs at a time; a hello that arrives while one runs is answered when that read ends. A message from a client that left before its `ready` was sent is dropped unhandled.
    - The window sends `looked` when it loses focus and when its page is hidden or unloaded, while it is connected. It keeps no record of its own (`## Grounding`, the row on the phone's clause).
    - The record is one for the daemon and not one per client, which `frame/questions.md` writes down as an assumption.
19. **The summary screen** says what changed since the person last looked, with no terminal on it.

The window works the changes out once, at `ready`, whose counts are the ones read at that hello (item 18), and holds them until the next load.
    - A count that is `null` in the record or in the `ready` is no difference in the count: a count the daemon could not read, or had not read within its wait, says nothing of a change. The comparison reads `WorkItem.newCommits` of both sides for this, since `inboxLine` gives no text for `null` and for zero alike.
    - The record's sessions are read with the record's work items and the present sessions with the present work items, each pair through `inboxLine`. No session of one side is matched to a session of the other: a session's id starts again at 1 with the daemon. Work items are matched by id, which the run folder keeps.
    - The summary screen opens at a load whose `ready` carries a look and at least one changed work item. Where `lastLook` is `null`, or no work item changed, it is skipped and the inbox screen opens (`frame/questions.md` Q4 and Q5).
    - The screen holds the bar of item 2 and, under it, at the window's width: the summary's sentence, which is the largest text of the screen; the sentence of when the person was last here, as in "You were last here 2 h 40 min ago."; one line per changed work item; and the control "Open the inbox".
    - The summary's sentence counts the changed work items by their present state, in the order of item 3 and in its words, as in "1 waiting for you, 1 finished". Where no changed work item has a state, it reads "1 work item changed" or "2 work items changed".
    - A line holds the title, the branch, one sentence of what changed, and the seal's place of item 15. The sentence opens with the present state: "Waits for you.", "Is working.", "Finished.", or "No agent is running." Where the work item is not in the record, "New since you were last here." follows, and where its count of new commits rose, the rise follows, as in "2 new commits since you were last here." The lines stand in the order of the sentence, and the ones with no state last.
    - The control "Open the inbox", and Enter, open the inbox screen: one step. A press on a line opens its work item's view. The screen offers no pane, no terminal and no "Ask" section.
    - On the inbox screen, for the rest of that load, the dot of a line whose work item changed is filled and the dot of the others is a ring, and the list ends with "A filled dot marks what changed since you were last here." Where `lastLook` is `null` every dot is filled and the list has no such sentence. The sentence of when the person was last here stands under the inbox's sentence as well, where a look is recorded.

## Data & interfaces

`apps/desktop/src/shared/ipc.ts` is unchanged. The protocol and the daemon change for *Scope* item 18 alone:

- `apps/desktop/src/shared/protocol.ts`: `ClientMessage` gains `{ type: "looked" }`. A new `Look` is `{ at: number; sessions: SessionInfo[]; workItems: WorkItem[] }`. The `ready` of `ServerMessage` gains `lastLook: Look | null`.
- `apps/desktop/src/daemon/`, as phase 3 built it at `24c2204`: `daemon.ts` handles `looked` and sends `lastLook`. The record is the class `LastLook` of the new `last-look.ts`, with `get()` and `record(at, sessions, workItems)`. It writes `last-look.json` into the run folder with mode 0600, to a partial file it then renames, and keeps the look in memory alone where the daemon has no run folder. `main.ts` changes in two comments that name what the run folder holds. `apps/desktop/test/daemon.test.ts` gains the cases of scenario 19, and the new `apps/desktop/test/last-look.test.ts` holds the module's own.
- `apps/desktop/src/daemon/daemon.ts`, in phase 4: the hello's answer waits for the read of the counts, for the wait of *Scope* item 18 at most, which a new option of `DaemonOptions` sets for the cases. `apps/desktop/test/daemon.test.ts` gains the cases of scenario 25. The option is `readyWaitMs`, 500 by default.
- The `ready` of `protocol.ts` types `lastLook` as always there. The window reads it as `message.lastLook ?? null`, for the `ready` of a daemon of an earlier build.

`apps/desktop/src/main/main.ts` changes the window's size and ground (*Scope* item 13).

The decisions are functions of `apps/desktop/src/renderer/view.ts`, with cases in `apps/desktop/test/view.test.ts`. `renderer.ts` draws what they return.

- `inboxLine`: unchanged. The preview, the view and `changesSince` call it as the line does.
- `inboxSummary(lines: readonly InboxLine[]): string`, new (*Scope* item 3).
- `inboxGroups(entries)`, new: the entries, each carrying its `line`, in the groups and the order of *Scope* item 2, each group with its heading from `stateHeading`.
- `stateHeading(state)`, `selectedLine(ids, selected)` and `inboxKey(event, context)`, new: a group's heading in the words of *Scope* item 2, the selection *Scope* item 17 keeps, and whether a key event is J, K, Enter or nothing under the conditions of *Scope* item 17.
- `moveSelection(ids: readonly number[], selected: number | undefined, key: "next" | "previous"): number | undefined`, new: the work item *Scope* item 17 selects, over the ids in the displayed order.
- `changesSince(look: Look, workItems: readonly WorkItem[], sessions: readonly SessionInfo[], paneKinds: readonly PaneKindInfo[])`, new: the changed work items of *Scope* item 19 in the order of its lines, each with its present `InboxLine` and its sentence. It matches work items by id and no session by id, and it reads a `null` count as *Scope* item 19 says.
- `summarySentence(changes): string` and `lastHere(at: number, now: number): string`, new: the two sentences of *Scope* item 19.
- `paneTabs(workItem: WorkItem, sessions: readonly SessionInfo[], held: ReadonlySet<number>): SessionInfo[]`, new: the sessions of *Scope* item 5's first sub-item, in the order of their ids. `held` is the ids of the panes this window holds a terminal for.
- `paneInFront(tabs: readonly SessionInfo[], last: number | undefined): number | undefined`, new: the id of *Scope* item 5's second sub-item, or nothing where there is no tab. Where no tab is alive, it is the most recent tab.
- `tabLabel(session: SessionInfo, paneKinds: readonly PaneKindInfo[]): string`: loses its `workItems` parameter and the title before the pane.
- `notificationFor`: unchanged in what it decides. For a pane without a work item its title is the new `tabLabel`, which is the text it gives today.
- `askButton(prompt: string, workItem: WorkItem)`: the work item is always there, so the branch for a missing one and its title go.
- `headlessLines(held: readonly HeadlessInfo[], workItemId: number)`: gives the lines of that work item alone.
- `agentKind`, `workItemFields`, `withHeadless`: unchanged.

In `apps/desktop/src/renderer/index.html` the three screens are three elements of `body`, of which the two not in front carry `hidden`. The status line and the "New work item" dialog are outside all three. The emblem in the bar is an `img` whose source is the copy of the drawing in `dist/`, which the page's content security policy allows as it allows the page's own stylesheet (`default-src 'self'`). An unsealed seal's place is an element the page's style draws, with no image.
