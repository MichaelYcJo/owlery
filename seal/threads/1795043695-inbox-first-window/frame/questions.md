- A session that starts to wait is notified while the inbox screen or the summary screen is in front of a focused window (`frame/design.md` *Scope* item 11).
- The look is one record for the daemon and not one per client. Only the desktop window sends it before the phone of v0.2 (`frame/design.md` *Scope* item 18).
- The seal's place says "No seal yet", as phase 1 builds it and variant 3 writes it, where variant 4 writes "Not sealed yet" (`frame/design.md` *Scope* item 15).
- The window opens at the mockups' size, 1440 pixels by 900 (`frame/design.md` *Scope* item 13).
- The view's right column is 420 pixels wide in both themes, between variant 3's 320 and variant 4's 548, and the seal's place is at its foot (`frame/design.md` *Scope* item 4).
- The sidebar's "Inbox" counts the work items, its "Summary" the work items that changed at this load, and its bell the ones that wait. The mockups' README leaves what they count open (`frame/design.md` *Scope* items 19 and 20).
- The words of the last look and of the inbox's sentence stay as phases 2 and 4 built them, "You were last here 2 h 40 min ago." and "1 waiting for you, 2 working", where variant 3 writes "You were away for 3 h 12 min, since 14:02" and "2 waiting for you · 10 work items" (`frame/design.md` *Scope* items 3 and 19).
- The sealed style of a seal's place is built in release 0.0.1 though no work item can set it, so that the owner sees the wax seal in both themes and milestone 2 sets one attribute (`frame/design.md` *Scope* item 15).
- The filter chooses one state or all, and the bell's press sets it to "Waiting for you" (`frame/design.md` *Scope* items 20 and 21).
- A daemon keeps 2000 characters of an agent's text, 20 commits and 50 files per work item (`frame/design.md` *Scope* item 23).
