**Departures from the frame and decisions made while building, each with its grounds.** Each was judged against `frame/design.md` *Scope* items 1, 18 and 19 and *Data & interfaces*.

- *The bar is one element, outside the three screens.* Item 19 says the summary screen "holds the bar of item 2", and item 2 puts the bar at the top of the inbox screen. Two bars would need two controls that open the form, and the form's control is found by its id (`trail/phases/phase-2.md`, its last paragraph). The one bar is hidden while a view is in front. The form therefore opens from the summary screen too.
- *No screen is shown before `ready`.* The inbox screen was displayed, empty, from the page's load. A hello is now answered after the read of the counts, so an inbox shown first would be on screen for that time before a summary took its place. The bar alone is displayed until `ready`.
- *One read of the counts runs at a time, and a hello joins the read under way.* Item 18 says the daemon "reads every work item's count when a client says hello". A hello that arrives during a read waits for that read, for the same 500 milliseconds at most, and starts none of its own. A second read beside the first would run git twice for the same answer, and where git hangs each hello would leave one more process behind. The cost is a commit that lands in the moment between a read's git call and a hello that joins it: that `ready` misses it, and the next read's `inbox` carries it.
- *A hello's read counts as the timer's.* The next timed read comes an interval after it. A timer already arranged is left to fire when it was due.
- *Nothing is sent to a client between its hello and its `ready`.* A count the hello's own read changes goes to the other clients in an `inbox`, as item 18 says. The client that said hello has it in its `ready`. An `inbox` sent to it first would arrive at a window that holds no kinds of pane yet.
- *The daemon handles nothing a client sent where the client left before its `ready`.* Item 18 says a message sent after the hello "is handled after its `ready` is sent" and says nothing of a `ready` that is never sent. A `start` handled for a client that is gone would open a pane nobody was told of, and a `looked` would record a look of lists that client was never sent. `gaps.md` *Not done* names it, because a message was handled as it arrived before.
- *The wait's option is `readyWaitMs`,* with 500 where it is absent. *Data & interfaces* asks for "a new option of `DaemonOptions`" and names none.
- *`lastHere` says "less than a minute" under one, minutes alone under an hour, hours and minutes under a day, hours alone where no minute is over, and days from then on,* as in "You were last here 3 days ago." A look the clock puts after now reads as less than a minute. Item 19 gives the one example, "2 h 40 min ago".
- *A count that fell is a change, and no rise is named.* Item 19 says a work item changed where the count differs, and that the rise follows "where its count of new commits rose". A branch that was reset gives a line that says the present state alone.
- *A work item the look does not hold names no rise.* Its sentence ends "New since you were last here.", as scenario 20 says.
- *`summarySentence` leaves out a changed work item with no state where another has one,* as the inbox's sentence does. Item 19 gives the words for the case where none has a state.
- *Enter on the summary screen is read under the inbox's conditions, through `inboxKey`.* The window tells the function that the inbox is in front while the summary is, and acts on Enter alone there: J and K do nothing. The keyboard starts on "Open the inbox", so Enter is that control's own press at first, and the screen's key once the focus is elsewhere. Item 19 says "The control 'Open the inbox', and Enter, open the inbox screen". A function of its own would repeat the four guards phase 2 pinned.
- *The window sends `looked` on three events: the window's `blur`, the document's `visibilitychange` where it is hidden, and `pagehide`,* each only while its connection is open. Item 18 says "when it loses focus and when its page is hidden or unloaded, while it is connected".
- *An unchanged line's dot is a ring 2 pixels thick in the muted text's colour, with no fill,* as artboard B1 draws it. The state's colour is the filled dot's alone. The dot's accessible name and title gain "changed since you were last here" or "not changed since you were last here" where a look is recorded, so the mark is not said by a shape alone.
- *The list's last sentence and the sentence of the last look are shown wherever a look is recorded,* a load that skipped the summary among them. Every dot is a ring then. The last sentence is left out of an inbox with no work item.
- *The summary screen takes from artboard A1 its arrangement and its measures:* the sentence at 60 pixels over a line height of 64, the last look at the right end of the same row, lines 72 pixels high with a rule under each, and a column 400 pixels wide for what changed. It takes B's colours, B's three typefaces and B's amber button.
