**Decisions made while building, each with its grounds.** Each was judged against `frame/design.md` *Scope* items 2 to 5, 9, 13, 15 and 17 and against the two artboards.

- *The dot is the line's state.* Item 2 gives a line five parts and has the heading say the state in words. The dot carries the state's class, and its accessible name and title are the heading's words, so a line read without its heading still says its state.
- *`finished` is `#C5CAD3`, a line with no state `#7B8496`, and a failed headless session `#F0766B`.* Direction B draws no finished agent and no line without one. The first is B's second text colour and the second the colour of B's dashed ring: neither asks for the person as amber and blue do. The third is the red of B's "Seal broken" heading. The owner has not seen them, and `gaps.md` carries that.
- *A count of new commits is said where there is one, and nothing where there is none,* in the preview and in the view's right column as on the line. `inboxLine` gives an empty text for no new commit, and the window adds no words of its own.
- *The preview shows the seal's place and the new commits on one row* under the branch. B1 fills that part of the preview with the question, the approval line and the commits by hash, which item 16 leaves out.
- *On a line the count of new commits is in the fixed-width typeface, after the branch,* as B1 draws it. In the preview and in the view it is a caption in the text typeface, as B's headings are. Item 13 keeps the fixed-width typeface for "a branch, a commit and the terminal".
- *A press on a line opens its view, and the work item a person leaves is the selected one when the inbox returns.* Item 17 keeps the selection by id and does not say what a visit to a view does to it. The keyboard returns to that line, as in phase 1, so the selection and the keyboard agree.
- *The keyboard's place on a line selects it.* A person who reaches a line with the Tab key sees it in the preview. Item 17 rules out the pointer's hover and says nothing of the focus. Where the keyboard is on a line, J and K move it with the selection, so Enter there opens the selected line. A line has no hover style.
- *Enter on a control is that control's press.* With the focus on "New work item", Enter opens the form and not the selected work item. `inboxKey` gives nothing for Enter there and still reads J and K. A modifier key is Control, Command, Option or Shift.
- *`select` marks the selected line without drawing the list again.* A J or a K therefore keeps the keyboard's place. The list is still drawn whole at every `inbox`, and the selection survives that by its id.
- *The list at the left of a view is in the order of the inbox's groups,* which is "the inbox's order" of item 4 as the screen shows it. It is drawn with the inbox, at `ready`, at every `inbox` and when a view opens.
- *The buttons of the pane kinds read "+ Shell" and so on, in B's quiet style,* where B2 writes one control, "Open a terminal". Item 5 asks for one button per kind the daemon offers.
- *The return control reads "‹ Inbox" and the preview's control ends with "›".* B draws both marks as small pictures. A character needs no drawing taken from the artboard.
- *The terminal reads its colours, its typeface and its size from the page's properties* (`look` in `renderer.ts`), so the look stays in one block. `--font-terminal` names JetBrains Mono first and then the Nerd Fonts and the Korean fallbacks the terminal had, so the icons of a shell prompt still draw.
- *The row of tabs scrolls sideways,* and the buttons of the pane kinds keep their width. `gaps.md` *Not done* had left the row's layout to this phase.
- *The sentence's element is hidden while the sentence is empty,* so the list holds no empty heading.
- *The window is 1440 by 900 with its frame.* The page inside it measured 1440 by 868 in the drive. Item 13 says "the window".
- *Not every measure is a property.* The scale of spaces, the sizes of the type, the widths of the columns and the heights of the bar, a control and a line are properties of `:root`. The values B gives to one rule alone stay literal in that rule, among them the 18 pixels above a group's heading, the 22 pixels above a view's header, the line heights of the three titles and the widths of the borders. `gaps.md` *Not done* names it.
