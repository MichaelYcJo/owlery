# Changelog: a pane's chain stops at the round ceiling

Issue #246. Where the daemon runs the owl chain, a round at its ceiling of rounds, or a later round, that leaves a finding open stops the chain with no judgment, and the person's next start runs one fix pass and one more round. A pane's chain command had no such stop. `owed` handed a fix pass at every round, and `fix-pass` recorded one at every round. Since #236, a finding located in the work item's records alone stops no chain through the convergence judgment. So a pane whose routing answers `Automation` `yes`, with nobody watching, had no bound on its rounds except a judgment made by the same session that builds the fixes.

- **`owed` stops at the ceiling.** Where the records owe a fix pass on the record of a round at the ceiling or after it, and that record holds an open finding, `owed` prints `{"step":"ceiling","round":<round>,"ceiling":<ceiling>,"open":[…]}` with exit code 0. It writes no prompt and no rules file, and commits nothing. The command asks the daemon's own rule, `chain.ts#gateBefore`, and reads the daemon's ceiling, `chain.ts#ROUNDS_CEILING`.
- **The person's word passes one round's stop.** `owed` and `fix-pass` take `--persons-word <round>`. Where the value names the round the stop stands on, `owed` hands that round's fix pass and `fix-pass` records it. A word for another round passes nothing, so a word given at one round's stop does not pass the next round's. A value that is no round number in digits is refused with the usage, and the usage names the option on both subcommands.
- **`fix-pass` records no pass that the ceiling stops.** Without the word, it exits with 1 and commits nothing. Its sentence names the round and the ceiling's round, and says that the same call with `--persons-word <round>` records the pass once the person has said to go on. The pass's commits and close blocks stay in the worktree for that call.
- **The stop holds under `Automation` `yes` as well.** Nobody watches such a run, and that is the case the ceiling bounds.
- **The pane's document says what the step means.** `skills/seal/SKILL.md` of the role bundle gains a row for `ceiling` in *The loop*. At that step, the session tells the person the round, its open findings and what the rounds kept finding, and runs nothing more of the chain. It passes `--persons-word` only once the person has said in this run, after that stop, to go on. *What you judge* has the session judge convergence only below the ceiling, since a fix pass handed on the person's word has the person's word as its answer. *A run under `Automation` `yes`* says the stop is no question and takes no default. `agents/orchestrator.md` now says that the ceiling bounds findings located in the records alone both where the daemon runs the chain and in a pane.
- **The daemon's code does not change.** Its chain still stops at its ceiling, and a person's start still runs one fix pass and one more round there.
- **What no run has shown yet.** No model was run on the new text. Whether a real pane's session stops at the step `ceiling`, tells the person, and passes `--persons-word` only after the person's word is the owner's walk's to show (#184). The command cannot tell whether the person said to go on. The bound is that a session cannot go on by doing nothing: it has to type the person's word in a command the person can read in the pane (`frame/questions.md` Q1).
