# Changelog: the summary leads with the seals given and broken since you last looked

Issue #22. When you came back, the summary said what changed while you were away and nothing of seals. A seal given or broken while you were away did not open it, and each line kept what the window read when it loaded, so a line could show a lit seal after the seal broke.

- **A seal given or broken since you last looked opens the summary.** It opens when the window loads, as a work item that came to need you opens it. A seal the daemon reads only after the window loaded, as just after the daemon started, joins the summary when it is read. The screen in front stays, and the count beside "Summary" in the sidebar grows.
- **The summary counts the seals first, and each work item once.** Its sentence reads, for example, "2 sealed, 1 seal broken, 1 needs you, 1 working". A work item whose seal was given is counted as sealed, and one whose seal broke as a broken seal, whatever state its line is in. The decisions made without asking still follow after a full stop.
- **The lines lead with the seals.** The work items whose seal was given come first, then the ones whose seal broke, then the others as before. A sealed line says, for example, "Sealed at 1a2b3c4 after 2 review rounds.", with the review rounds its records hold, and leaves the count out where they hold none. A broken line says "Seal broken." and the seal's own sentence, for example "Seal broken. `src/app.ts` changed after the sealed commit `1a2b3c4`." Each line carries the work item's seal.
- **A seal that broke after the work item's pull request merged is not said in the summary.** The line says "Merged", as in the inbox.
- **Each line says the work item as it stands now.** Which work items the summary lists is chosen when the window loads. Each line's words and seal, and the summary's sentence, are worked out again each time the summary is drawn. The summary in front is drawn again when the lists change, and the keyboard stays where it was. So a seal that breaks while you read the summary turns its line to "Seal broken." in place. A work item that no longer needs you stays listed and says its state. One you archived leaves the summary, and the count beside "Summary" counts the lines the summary draws.
- **The main control says why its work item waits.** "Answer the first question" opens the first line that waits on a question, a call to allow or its agent's own question. Where none does and a line waits for another reason, such as a broken seal, a stopped chain or a failed check, the control reads "Open the first that needs you" and opens that line. The hint beside it says which line Enter opens: "Enter opens the first question." or "Enter opens the first that needs you.", since a line whose seal broke can stand before the first question.
- **A seal the daemon has not read yet is no change.** Where the daemon had not read a work item's branch at your last look, or has not read it now, as just after it starts again, the seal is not read as given or broken. A last look written by the build before this one holds no seals, so the first load after the update shows no seal as given or broken.
- **`pnpm run screenshots` captures the summary.** It writes eight views, sixteen files in Night watch's two appearances. A second client of the daemon records a look before the scene's seals are given, and the window loads again once the scene stands, so the summary leads with a seal given and a seal broken.
- **For a client of the daemon:** a work item carries no `seal` until the daemon has read its branch since it started, nor does one that records no commit its branch was cut at. `null` then says that the branch's newest commit holds no seal. The last look keeps each work item's `seal` as the daemon sent it, `null` among it, and leaves it out where the work item carried none. No field and no message is added.
