**Constraints.**

- No state lives on the UI's side, so the window keeps no look of its own and works every change out from the look of its hello.
- No message carries a session's id, and no field names a vendor. This work adds no field and no message.
- A page that leaves sends `looked` (`renderer.ts:1684`), so a case that needs a seal between a look and a load writes the look into the run folder before the start.
- No case reaches GitHub or starts a real `claude`, and the built app starts in the background.

**The failure scenario of the chosen approach.** In six months the first failure a person meets is the one this work leaves to the owner: the window stays open for days, the page never loads again, and no summary ever opens by itself, though the daemon records a look at every loss of focus. `frame/questions.md` Q1 holds the choice. The second: a daemon that ran without a client reads a moved head more slowly than `ready` waits, `ready` carries the seal of the earlier read, and a seal that broke while the app was closed is on the work item's line in the inbox, in #44's notification, and in no summary. #44's phase 1 measured one reading of a branch with 10 round records at 612 milliseconds, over the 500 `ready` waits, so a work item with that many rounds meets this at each such commit (`frame/questions.md` Q4). The third: the changed set is the load's and the lines are the present, so after a day a line of the summary says a state that nothing of the look explains, as in "Is working." alone. The fourth: `view.ts` holds one more rule of what a change is, the seal's table beside the state, the count and the decisions, and a field added to `inboxLine` by a later work item opens a summary at every load unless the look copies it or `changesSince` gives the look's side the present value, as #44's build does for the seal. No case of this work holds a later field to that rule. The fifth: git fails at one read of a seal, the daemon sends null until the head moves again, and a look taken meanwhile holds null for a sealed work item, so the next reading says the seal was given.
