# Questions for the planner: 1795397381-summary-of-seals

The issue and `routing.md` left these judgments open, and the tree and the policy answered each. Nobody is asked them again: each line names where its grounds stand.

- What counts as a change to sealed or broken since the look: `frame/design.md` *Scope* item 3, and `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows "A null on either side says nothing of the seal, as a null count of commits does" and "A seal that broke under a merged pull request is no change of the seal".
- How a side with no seal reading is compared, which #44's build answers for the look's side: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows "A side without a seal reading is read with the other side's seal" and "The seal is left out of both sides where one has no reading".
- What the summary compares, and where it is kept: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows "The look keeps each work item's seal reading, and the window compares" and "The daemon works out which seals changed and sends the result".
- What a daemon that has not read a seal yet sends, and what the summary makes of it: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows "`seal` is absent until the daemon's first read, and null says the head holds no seal", "`seal` stays null until the first read, as #44 builds it", "Null is also taken off a read at which git failed" and "A seal the daemon read after `ready` joins the set once".
- How the summary leads with the sealed work items and the broken ones, and how its sentence counts them: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows "The summary's sentence counts the seals first and each work item once", "The seals are counted apart, after a full stop, as the decisions are" and "The line's state is replaced by the product's states".
- Whether the summary may go on saying what it said at the load: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows "The changed set is the load's, and each line says the present", "The changes stay as the load worked them out", "The set is worked out again at every `inbox` as well" and "Only the seal's place is the present one".
- What the main control opens and says once a broken seal makes a line wait: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows "The main control says why its work item waits, and a question comes before a broken seal" and the row that opens "The main control opens the first line that waits".
- How the summary says that a work item moved from review to its seal, which the issue's comment asks: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row "The count of rounds in a sealed line's sentence is `recorded.rounds`".
- What of the artboards A1, C1 and C2 this work takes: `frame/design.md` *Grounding*, the paragraph on the mockups, and *Out of scope*, the items on C2's view, on when a seal was given and on the pull request's words.
- Whether the pull request carries a screenshot of the summary, and how `docs/design.md` comes to list it: `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the rows "The screenshot run gains the summary as a view" and "The sentences of `docs/design.md` on the run's views change through `## Rule changes`".

This assumption is written down and not asked, because either answer changes a line of the build: the sidebar's count beside "Summary" counts the lines the summary would draw now, so it falls when a work item of the changed set is archived.
