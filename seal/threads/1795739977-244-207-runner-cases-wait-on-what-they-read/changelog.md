# Changelog: built-app cases wait for what a dialog asked the daemon before they press or read in it

Issues #244 and #207. On the hosted runner, some cases of the built app failed now and then on a first attempt and passed on the second. A dialog that asks the daemon for its content draws the answer after it opens, and a case that pressed or read in it before the answer came pressed where a button stood a moment earlier, or noted lists that were still being drawn. In `work-item-menu.test.ts` a dialog the failed case left open then kept every later case of the file from opening a dialog or a menu. The old-CLI case of `role-bundle.test.ts` lost its notice where a read of the CLI's version passed the case's hold before the case wrote it, read the version the case had just rewritten, and ended before the page loaded anew connected to the daemon.

- **The removal case and the register case of `work-item-menu.test.ts` wait for the answer drawn.** The removal case notes the lists once every work item's loss stands in the dialog. The register case presses "Not now" once the records dialog shows the files of its plan, and asserts their paths.
- **The other places of the same kind wait too.** A press of Create in the "New work item" form waits for the plan of the name typed, not for any plan, through one helper, `formPlanOf` in `built-app.ts`. A press of Cancel right after the form opens waits for the plan the opening asked for. A press of Cancel in the delete dialog waits for the loss. The case of the form's setup sets the folder and sets it back in one call, so no plan of the text it typed in between is asked. Each other place the census found was left as it was, and `trail/phases/phase-3.md` says why for each.
- **A case that fails leaves no dialog and no menu open for the next case of its file.** `atRestAfterFailure` in `built-app.ts` presses Escape after a failed case until nothing stands open, and throws with what stays open where Escape closes nothing. After a case that passed it does nothing, so a passing case that leaves a dialog open still fails the next. The files whose cases share one app and open a dialog or a menu register it, and `daemon-restart.test.ts`, whose cases quit the app, does not.
- **A wait that fails names where the last press landed, where it missed.** Its message names the selector the last click aimed at and the element its press landed on, or that no press reached the page.
- **The old-CLI case holds every read of the version, whenever it began.** The stand-in for the CLI reads the version first and prints it once the case's hold is gone, and the case puts the new version in place whole. Where the case fails, its message says what the status line read and in which page.
- **Nothing in the app changes.** The work changes test files only.
