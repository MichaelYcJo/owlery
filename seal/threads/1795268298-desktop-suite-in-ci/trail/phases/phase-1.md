# Phase 1: 1795268298-desktop-suite-in-ci

## What this phase found

**No frame was written, so the issue's two *Done when* lines were the frame.** `routing.md` declares `Planning | the session`, and the work item has no `frame/`. The workflow at `f51dae9` already met the first line. The second line held only once the suite passed on the runner, which is what this phase built.

**The 18 failures of runs 44909892650 and 44910365847 had one cause: the runner's screen.** Run 44913631354 measured it through Electron's `screen` module: the display is 1024 by 768, its work area 1024 by 674, and the app's 1440 by 900 window is made 1024 by 674. `system_profiler SPDisplaysDataType` printed nothing there. In that window the sidebar (256 px) and the facts column (420 px) keep their widths, so the pane area has about 300 px.

- `terminal-basics`: the tab strip clipped the second pane's tab, so `app.click` at the middle of its `×` landed on the "+ Shell" button and opened a third pane. Every later case then counted panes the first failure left behind: "Shell 4", "Shell 7", the empty reads, "pane 7 did not start its process". The wrap case computed `columns + 20 - 94` with 42 columns and threw `Invalid count value: -32`.
- `work-item-menu`: the removal dialog with two work items is taller than 674 px, so `#remove-submit` stood below the page's foot and the click missed. The dialog stayed open and modal, and the next two cases found no menu.

Both files were seen red locally with the same cases: the window shrunk to 1024 by 743 through `evaluateMain` gave the 15 failures of `terminal-basics` and the 3 of `work-item-menu`, at `ec86295` (executed). With the fix and the same shrink both files passed, 54 cases (executed).

**The fix gives a background start its window's own size, judged against `docs/` having no rule on window size and `src/main/main.ts` naming 1440 by 900 "the size the screens are drawn for".** The cases assume a window of that size, and that assumption is the machine's, not what they check. The first fix, `736e3ea`'s `BuiltApp.atWindowSize`, laid the page out at 1440 by 900 with `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride` inside the smaller window. On the orchestrator's proposal it was replaced at `eb59857`: `createWindow` sets `enableLargerThanScreen: background`, so a start under `OWLERY_BACKGROUND=1`, a test's or an agent's, keeps its window at 1440 by 900 on a smaller screen, and a person's start passes `false`, Electron's default, as before (read). Locally the option let `setBounds` make a window 2400 by 1500 on a 1728 by 1023 work area, where without it the height stayed at 1023 (executed, a probe that was removed). `BuiltApp.start` now fails a background start whose page is not 1440 by 900, and the narrow-window case of `work-item-menu` gives the page back with `clearDeviceMetricsOverride` as before the work. The size lives in `src/main/window-size.ts`, read by `src/main/main.ts` and by the helper, so the fact has one home (`skills/core/SKILL.md` *The rule a mechanism is kept against*). Run 44919934535 at `eb59857` passed the size check at every start on the runner, and none of the 18 failures came back.

**`seal mutate` cannot fail `enableLargerThanScreen` on the owner's Mac**, because the screen there holds 1440 by 900 with or without the option. Its red is the runner's runs: 18 failed at `ec86295`, and none of them failed at `eb59857`. `seal mutate` making the window 1400 wide turned `test/keep-awake.test.ts` red at the start's size check, `the window's page is 1400 by 900 and not 1440 by 900` (executed).

**A second cause appeared once the size held: `test/daemon.test.ts` "sends a reconnecting client each line once" timed out in run 44912086303.** Its shell paused 20 ms after each of forty lines, and each pause starts a `sleep` process. On the runner the loop alone took 2.56 s and 3.05 s with the runner idle (runs 44912693366 and 44913122661), against 0.8 s of pauses. The case now prints five bursts of eight lines 100 ms apart, so it starts five processes. That loop takes 0.59 s locally. The case still prints lines before and after its attach, 150 ms after the client's ready. `seal mutate` doubling the replay's text in `src/daemon/daemon.ts` turned the case red with `expected 2 to be 1` (executed), and the file was restored by the tool.

**The Open in pointer case of `work-item-menu` still fails at most first starts of the app in a job, and its cause was narrowed and not fixed.** Every failure stands at the same check: the one after the pointer crossed "Copy path" on its way back to "Finder" (line 369 as the case stands), and not at the first check after "Finder" as the first account of this record said. It failed in runs 44911168018, 44914578391 (first attempt), 44915064055, 44915417928, 44915771624, 44919934535 and 44920357234, and in the first measuring run of 44912086303 and 44912693366. It passed in the second attempt of 44914578391, in 44911582682, 44913122661, 44913631354 and 44914077600, at every later start in a job, and at every local run. Three things were learned. First, the emulated page was not the cause: the case failed with a real 1440 by 900 window in 44919934535. Second, the crossing step leaves the submenu standing only where the pointer reaches "Finder" within the 300 ms pause, and the case made three calls through the debugging port between the two moves. Sending the second move with no call between them still failed, in 44920842898 at `d4fe609`, so at a first start the page took longer than the pause to handle two moves sent one after the other. Third, two moves sent together are joined into one: the case then passed with the pause removed (`seal mutate`, SURVIVED, executed), so that form pinned nothing. Why the page is that slow at a first start was not measured. Both edits of the case were taken back, and the case stands as before the work.

The temporary diagnostic step and the recording code were removed before the hand-back. `.github/workflows/check.yml` is as `f51dae9` wrote it, and `test/work-item-menu.test.ts` is as `eb59857` wrote it.
