**Decisions the frame left to the code.** Each is judged against the item of `frame/design.md` *Scope* it names.

- **An archive is not refused where a pane outlives the wait** (`src/daemon/daemon.ts`, the case `archiveWorkItem`, items 17 and 21). Item 21 has the daemon kill, stop and wait for an archive as for a delete, and names a delete and a repository's removal alone as what is then refused. The archive waits the 3 seconds of `frame/questions.md` Q2 and then archives the work item, whether or not each pane has exited. A pane that has not is listed as running under the archived work item until its process ends, and as exited after. `Daemon#endSessions` takes the sentence it refuses with as its fourth argument, and null where it goes on.
- **Nothing starts in a work item while its archive waits** (`Daemon#archiving`, items 17 and 21). Item 17 gives a headless session the archive stopped the error "ended when the work item was archived". Those words are false wherever the archive is refused after the stop, which review round 3's finding 2 named for a removal. So nothing refuses an archive after it stopped a session: a `start` and an `ask` in the work item are refused while the archive waits, in the sentence item 17 gives for an archived one, and no session can then start that the person was not told of.
- **An archive does not run on `Daemon#changing`** (item 23). Item 23 names a rename of a branch, a delete, a removal and a creation, each of which takes or gives up a branch. An archive does neither, and behind a creation it would wait on git for nothing. Where a removal takes the work item out of the store while its archive waits, the archive is answered with "no work item <id>".
- **A request that changes nothing** (`frame/design.md` *Data & interfaces*, the sentence on `done`). An archive of a work item that is archived, or that another archive is ending the sessions of, and a restore of one that is not archived, are each answered with `done` and no `inbox`, as phase 2's `setWorkItem` is.
- **What an archived work item keeps of its branch** (item 17, `## Removes` row 7). `Daemon#readCommits` passes over it, at a hello's read too, and the counts, the commits and the changes last read stay, so the archived screen's preview shows them as they stood. One work item's read is now `Daemon#readBranch`, which `readCommits` calls for each work item that is not archived. A restore calls it for the restored one alone, after the `inbox` that carries the restore, and sends a second `inbox` where the read found a change (item 18, "reads the work item's branch again at once").
- **The store's part** (`src/daemon/repositories.ts`, item 1). `setWorkItem` takes `archivedAt`: a time archives, and null restores. The time is the daemon's clock, `DaemonOptions.now`.
- **The archived screen** (`src/renderer/list.ts`, `src/renderer/renderer.ts#openArchived`, item 18).
  - It is the `list-screen` template's third copy, `archived-screen`, and `held.archivedOf` holds the repository it was last opened on.
  - Its head reads "Archived in <the repository's name>".
  - What is typed in the search field narrows it, as it narrows the list in front on the other two screens. Its filter of states is hidden: an archive ends the work item's sessions.
  - A press of a line selects it and opens no view. J and K move the selection, the menu's key opens the selected line's menu, and Enter does nothing (`gaps.md`, *Not done*).
  - The preview's button reads "Restore" and sends the restore.
  - The screen stays in front after a restore, and says "No work item of this repository is archived." once it holds none.
  - Where the daemon no longer keeps the repository, the window puts the inbox in front.
- **The dialog before an archive** (`renderer.ts#archive`, `src/renderer/view.ts#archivalOf`, item 17). An archive of a work item none of whose sessions runs is sent at the menu's press, and a refusal is said in the status line. Otherwise `sessions-dialog` names each running session, a pane by its tab's label and a headless session as "Asked: <its prompt>", with the keyboard on "Cancel". It is drawn again at each `inbox` and each `headless`, so the sessions it sends at the press are the ones it then names. It closes where the window's list no longer holds the work item.
- **The menus** (`view.ts#workItemMenu`, item 6). Archive is a work item's last item, under a rule. The menu of an archived work item holds Restore, the items that open its folder, and the two copies. Delete joins both in phase 5.
- **No notification for a session of an archived work item** (`view.ts#notificationFor`, its sixth argument). `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered*, the row on one list with `archivedAt`, rejects a design in which a reader "raises its notification". `notificationFor` titled a session whose work item `workItems` does not hold by its tab's label, so a pane that outlives the wait would have raised one after its archive. It now gives none where `archived` holds the session's work item. No item of *Scope* asks for it.
