# Changelog: the seal has a back that sets each stage's cost beside the defects each round caught

Issue #47. A seal said that the work held and nothing of what the review caught or what the review cost. The round records kept each finding and the cost ledger kept each stage's cost, but nothing set the two side by side, so a person could not tell from their own numbers whether the seal was worth what it cost.

- **A sealed work item's view opens to the back of its seal.** The strip of tabs above the panes gains a tab, "Back of the seal", before the panes' tabs, for a work item that has a seal, sealed or broken, a recorded review round or a chain. A work item that has a seal, sealed or broken, opens with that tab in front, and one opened from a session's notification opens to that session's pane, as before. A tab you choose stays in front while the view stays, and a later list from the daemon moves no tab in front.
- **The back shows the seal and its record, then what each review round caught.** It draws the large seal with the words of its state, then the seal's record: the tree and its branch, the base, the item, the broad gate's commit, the count of rounds, who built, who judged, the model and the rules. Under the record, one sentence counts the defects caught and fixed, another counts the findings rejected, deferred and open where any is, and a third gives the findings of each round in order, for example "Findings by round: 3, 1, 0." Each round then lists its findings: the symptom, the severity, each place as `path:line`, and "Fixed in" with the commit, "Rejected", "Deferred to" its home, or "Open". A finding that reopens an earlier one, or is a regression of one, says which. A round that found nothing says so, with the findings it confirmed.
- **A defect caught and fixed is a finding closed as fixed that no later round reopened.** So a finding closed, reopened and closed again counts once, and a finding the fix pass rejected counts as no defect. Where the rounds hold no finding, the back says "The review rounds caught nothing."
- **Each stage's cost stands beside the round it ran for.** Each round lists the stages that ran for it: the round, the fix pass on it, and the orchestrator's judgment or ruling after it. The broad gate and the sealer follow the rounds. Each stage shows how it ended where it did not finish by itself, its time, its tokens in and out, its cache read and written, and its cost, with the word "converted" where the cost is a value converted at API prices. A stage that reported no cost says "No cost reported", and the gate, which is no agent's turn, shows none. The sums add the converted costs and the charged costs apart and never one to the other, count the stages that reported no cost, and say what a converted cost is. Sessions asked outside the chain are counted and summed in one line of their own. The calls each stage's role was refused, and the lines of a record or of the ledger that could not be read, are counted at the foot.
- **A chain stopped before its seal shows what it has cost so far.** The back stands under "No seal yet", with the rounds recorded and the stages run.
- **A broken seal's back stays whole and names the commit that broke it.** Under the seal's sentence stands "Broken by" with the commit and its subject: the oldest commit on the branch's own line after the sealed commit that changes a file other than the gate's record, `guarantee.md` and `trail/refusals.md`. A merge of the base is named by its merge commit. Where no such commit can be told, as where the branch no longer holds the sealed commit, the seal's own sentence says why.
- **The back reads the committed records and the daemon's ledger, and keeps nothing.** The findings, the seal's record and the refused calls are read as the branch's newest commit holds them, so a record you have not committed says nothing on the back. The costs are read from the ledger on your Mac. The window asks for both when the back comes in front, and lets them go when another tab comes in front or the view is left. It asks again when the work item's seal, its recorded rounds or its chain change, and draws nothing again for a list that changes none of them, so a selection in the back stays.
- **The pull request's body carries the same back.** Below the seal section, between the lines `<!-- owlery:back -->` and `<!-- /owlery:back -->`, the body holds the back of the seal under the heading "The back of the seal": the sentences that count the defects, each round's findings, one table of the stages with their time, tokens, cache and cost, the sums, and the lines on the sessions outside the chain, the refused calls and the lines not read. It holds no seal and no seal's record, which the seal section above it carries. Every reader of the pull request sees each stage's cost, with its "converted" word.
- **Each seal sent to an open pull request rewrites the back's section.** Owlery replaces the lines between the back's two markers, as it replaces the seal section, and puts the section after the seal section where the body holds none. A line you write inside the back's markers is lost at the next seal. A body that holds a marker of the back more than once, or an opening marker with no closing one after it, stops the stage with a sentence that says so, for example "The pull request stage failed: the body of pull request #4 holds 2 `<!-- owlery:back -->` lines, and Owlery replaces one section of the back of the seal."
- **The part "Review rounds" is gone from the body.** Each round's findings stand in the back's section, which each seal rewrites. A pull request opened by an earlier build of this release keeps that part until you remove it, and its first seal after the update puts the back's section after the seal section.
- **At GitHub's limit, the back's findings give way before its numbers.** Where a new body would pass 65,536 characters, the changelog, the gate's run and the decisions give way first, the last first, each to a line that names its record. Then each round's list of findings gives way, the last round first, to a line that names that round's record, for example "`seal/threads/<item>/trail/rounds/round-2.md` holds this round's findings." The table of the stages and the sums stay, since no committed record holds a cost. At an update of an open pull request the same rule holds for the back's findings, and no line outside Owlery's two sections is cut.
- **A marker line is read by one rule.** A line is a marker line of either section where it is the marker once the spaces and tabs at its ends are taken off, and no other character is taken off. A part of the body that quotes a marker of either section on a line of its own gives way to the line that names its record whatever the body's length, so the body holds each marker once. The pull request check reads the seal section alone, and no line of the back.
- **The screenshots of a finished and of a broken work item show the back.** In `pnpm run screenshots`, the finished work item's chain now runs two rounds: the first leaves one finding, a fix pass closes it, and the second confirms the fix, with a converted cost for each stage. Its view and the broken work item's view open to the back. The views of a working and of a parked work item show the back's tab behind the pane in front.
- **No real chain has drawn a back yet, and no pull request on GitHub carries one.** Every test ran through a fake agent and a stand-in for `gh`. Whether each stage of a real chain lands beside its round, and whether GitHub draws the back's section as the app's tests write it, is measured by the first pull request of Owlery's own that Owlery seals (issue #48).
- **After an update, the back reads "Reading the records." until the daemon restarts.** A daemon started by the build before this one does not answer the new message. Its ledger's entries name no chain's stage, so their costs stand on the line of the sessions outside the chain.
- **For a client of the daemon:** a ledger entry carries `chain`: the kind of the chain's stage it is for (`round`, `fix-pass`, `judgment`, `ruling`, `gate`, `seal` or `pull-request`) and its `round`, or null for a session no chain started. A line with no `chain` is read with `chain` null. A new message `readSealRecords`, with `requestId` and `workItemId`, is answered with `sealRecords`: `head`, the commit the records were read at; `record`, the seal's record (`commit`, `branch`, `base`, `item`, `gate`, `rounds`, `builtBy`, `judgedBy`, `model`, `rules`) or null where the newest verdict is no seal; `brokenBy`, `commit` and `subject` of the commit that broke a broken seal, or null; `rounds`, each with `round`, `target`, `findings` (`number`, `says`, `severity`, `places` with `path` and `lines`, `close` with `verdict`, `commit`, `home` and `answer`, `reopens` and `regression`), `confirms` and `unread`; `refusals`, each with `kind`, `round` and `rule`; and `unreadRefusals`. The words a record holds are cut at 2,000 characters. A work item whose worktree holds no records directory of its own is answered with no record and no round, and an id the daemon lists no work item under, or a branch whose head the daemon cannot read, with an `error`.
