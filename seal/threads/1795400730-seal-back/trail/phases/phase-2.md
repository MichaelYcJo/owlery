# Phase 2: 1795400730-seal-back

## What this phase found

**The frame holds for this phase, with one interface changed.** Every seam `frame/design.md` *Scope* items 3 to 15 name stood at the build's base `c1e1deb` (read): `round-record.ts#Finding`, `#Close` and `locationPaths`; the `PLACE` grammar of `record-blocks.ts`; `seal-records.ts#readGuarantee` and `#readGate`; `chain-records.ts#lastRun`; `decision-record.ts#workItemDirectory` and `#declaredBranch`; `worktrees.ts#fileAt`, `#filesAt`, `#commitNamed` and `#holdsCommit`; `chain.ts#refusalsText`, `stageCell` and `CLOSING_RECORDS`; `seal-state.ts#workItemSeal`; and the `readLedger` case of `daemon.ts`. The interface that changed is the departure below.

**Departure: a refused call carries its stage as a kind and a round.** `frame/design.md` *Data & interfaces* gives `SealRecords.refusals` as `{ stage: string; rule: string }[]`, the stage as the row writes it. At HEAD it is `{ kind: ChainStageKind; round: number; rule: string }[]`. The composition names a refused call's stage with the same typed table as a ledger entry's stage, so the window and the body say "Ruling after round 2" for both. With the frame's type, `src/shared/seal-back.ts` would have to read `ruling 2` back into a kind, which is the inverse of `chain.ts#stageCell`, and the rule of that cell would then stand in two modules. At HEAD the reader `chain.ts#readRefusals` reads the cell with `cellStage`, which stands beside `stageCell` and uses the same `kindCell`, so the writer and its reader keep one rule in one module. *Scope* item 8 holds as written. Judged against `frame/design.md` *Scope* items 8 and 10, and plugin `skills/core/SKILL.md` *The rule a mechanism is kept against*, "A fact has one source".

**Decisions made while building, each with its grounds.**

- **The daemon cuts the strings, and the module gives them whole.** `seal-back-records.ts#sealRecords` returns each string whole. `daemon.ts#sealRecordsOf` cuts each at 2,000 characters where the daemon sends `sealRecords`, as `daemon.ts` cuts every other string it sends (`cutText`, and `sealAt`'s comment "the daemon cuts the sentence where it sends it"). So phase 4's body reads the records whole. Judged against `frame/design.md` *Data & interfaces*, "A string is cut at 2,000 characters".
- **The empty value is the daemon's.** `sealRecords` takes the directory as a path. Where `workItemDirectory` gives null, the daemon answers `noSealRecords(head)`, which holds the head and nothing else. Judged against *Scope* items 4 and 9.
- **The commit that broke a seal is read in `worktrees.ts`.** `worktrees.ts#firstCommitOutside` runs `git log --first-parent --reverse --format=%H%x00%s <sealed>..<head>` with one `:(top,exclude,literal)` pathspec for each closing record. Every other git call of the daemon stands in that module, so this one does too. Judged against *Scope* item 7.
- **The rounds of the seal's record are counted at the head where the sealed commit is missing.** *Scope* item 5 counts the round records the sealed commit holds. Where the repository holds no commit of the name the verdict gives, `git ls-tree` cannot read that commit, and the count is of the round records the head holds. The seal is then broken, and `seal-state.ts#sealAt`'s sentence already says why.
- **What the composition gives beyond *Scope* items 10 to 15, so that the window and the body draw and compose nothing themselves.** Each part is in `src/shared/seal-back.ts#Back`:
  - the line of a broken seal, "Broken by `<commit>`", with the commit's subject (*Scope* item 20);
  - one line for each stage that was refused a call, as "Round 1 was refused 3 calls: 2 by `no-write` and 1 by `no-command`." (*Scope* item 16);
  - the lines not read: "3 lines of round 2's record were not read.", "1 row of the refused calls was not read." and "1 line of the ledger was not read." (`frame/design.md` *Data & interfaces*, the words table).
- **A round the ledger names and no committed record holds has a part of its own.** A round's stage that failed commits no record, and its entry still names its round. Its part says "No record of round 2 is committed." and holds that stage's line. So no stage leaves the chain's sum without a line. Judged against *Scope* item 15, "the stages entered so far".
- **Wordings the frame does not fix.** `frame/questions.md` Q5 leaves the wording to the build, and phase 3 reads it drawn:
  - The head says "before the seal" only where the records hold a seal's record. Without one it says "The review rounds caught 1 defect that was fixed." Where findings stand and none is a defect it says "The review rounds caught no defect that was fixed before the seal."
  - A finding's reference reads "It reopens round 1's finding 1.", "It is a regression of round 1's finding 3." or "It is a regression of round 1.".
  - The record's row "Tree" reads "`3f2a9c1` on `feat/x`", with the branch where `routing.md` names one. A row whose value is null, and "Built by" where no builder is named, is left out.
  - A span is in whole minutes, floored, and an hour with no minutes reads "1 h".
  - A count of tokens that no model reported reads "none". A stage whose usage names no model has no tokens.
  - A stage whose usage reports no cost is counted with the stages that reported none, as one whose usage is null is.
  - The sentence on a converted cost stands only where a converted cost is shown. The frame's body example shows it with converted costs, and with charged costs alone it would explain a word the back does not show.
  - The sessions outside the chain that reported no cost read "1 session asked outside the chain reported no cost.".
  - `readSealRecords` for a branch whose head the daemon cannot read is refused with "Owlery cannot read the head of the branch <branch>.".
