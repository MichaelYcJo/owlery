**Departures from the frame.**

- **The back's section holds the lines on the sessions outside the chain, the refused calls and the lines not read.** *Scope* item 22 lists the head's sentences, the rounds' findings, the table, the sums and the sentence on a converted cost, and says the section "holds what item 10 gives". Item 10's `Back` also gives `outside`, `refused` and `unread`, and the window draws each of them (phase 3). So the section writes them after the sums, the refused calls as a list. Leaving them out would give the reader of the pull request a sum that leaves out sessions the window counts. Judged against *Scope* items 10 and 22.
- **A round's findings give way to a line that names the round's own record.** Item 24 and scenario 19 say "one line that names `trail/rounds/`". The line names `<directory>/trail/rounds/round-N.md` and says "holds this round's findings.", so a reader opens one file and not a folder. A round with no finding has no list, so it never gives way. Judged against *Scope* item 24.
- **The case "lists the round records in the order of their rounds" of `test/pull-request.test.ts` went with `BodyRecords.rounds`.** Its subject, the body's reading of the round records, is what item 23 removes. The order it pinned, round 10 after round 2, now comes from `seal-back-records.ts#roundFiles`, and no case held it there. A case of `test/seal-back-records.test.ts` now does, "gives the rounds in the order of their numbers".
- **The case of `test/pr-check.test.ts` that runs the check on the stage's body gives each turn a usage.** Scenario 16 names that case as the check on the body with the back, and a back with no cost shows no row with a cost. With a usage of $0.25 for each turn, the body holds a stage's row with its cost, and the check still passes it.

**Decisions made while building, each with its grounds.**

- **One list of columns heads the window's stages and the body's table.** `STAGE_COLUMNS` moved from `renderer/shared.ts` to `shared/seal-back.ts`, which both import. *Scope* item 22 says the table has "the columns of item 14", and one source keeps the two from drifting. Judged against `skills/core/SKILL.md`, "A fact has one source".
- **`markerOf` returns the marker it reads.** It returned whether the line was the seal's marker. With four markers, `withSection` needs to know which one a line is, and `marked` needs to know whether it is any. The rule is unchanged: only spaces and tabs are taken off the line's ends. Judged against *Scope* item 27.
- **One function replaces a section of either kind.** `withSection(body, section, which, after)` holds the counting, the refusals and the insertion, and `withSealSection` and `withBackSection` call it with the seal's or the back's markers. The back's section, where the body holds none, goes after the seal section's closing line with a blank line on each side. The refusal names "section of the back of the seal". Judged against *Scope* item 22, "as a body with such markers of the seal does".
- **The stage composes the back before it pushes, with no seal.** `runPullRequest` reads the ledger with `Ledger#read(item.id, item.createdAt)`, as `readLedger` reads it, and the records with `sealRecords(worktree, directory, head, null)`. The stage runs where the seal holds at the head, so no commit broke it, and the back's section carries no seal. Judged against *Scope* item 22.
- **A finding's line ends its symptom with a full stop where it ends with none.** The round record's heading holds no full stop, and the line goes on with its places, its severity and its close, so the symptom reads as its own sentence.
- **The body's free parts give way before the back's findings, and the findings by rounds, the last first.** `pullRequestBody` empties the free parts from the last, then raises the count of rounds whose findings give way while the body is over the limit. `withBackSection` raises the same count at an edit and cuts no other line. Judged against *Scope* item 24.
