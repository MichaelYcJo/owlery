What follows is each seam as the merged tree holds it at `cad4a64`, read on 2026-11-24. #22's merge moved no line this section names (`frame/design.md` *Grounding*, the last row of its third table), so each stands at `90184d5` as written. The build starts from `90184d5`. *Overlap with the release* says what #22's merge moved under this frame.

- **The ledger.** `src/daemon/ledger.ts#entryOf` reads a line as an entry only where every field of `LedgerEntry` is of its type, and `daemon.ts#enter` (line 1694) writes one entry for each headless session that ended, with `stage: info.role`. The gate's entry is added in `daemon.ts#runGate` (line 2609). The file is append-only and a deleted work item's lines stay, so the field this work adds has to be read as null where a line does not hold it.
- **The chain's run.** `daemon.ts#runStage(workItemId, kind, round, role, worktree, prompt, after)` (line 2297) starts a stage through `beginHeadless(held, prompt, role, worktree, first?)` (line 1630), which the `ask` case also calls (line 1247). The kind and the round are in `runStage`'s hands and are not passed on. `runConvergence` and `runRuling` both start their stage as `chain.ts#ORCHESTRATOR`, so the entry's pair is one for a judgment and a ruling.
- **The round record's reader.** `src/daemon/round-record.ts#readRoundRecord` reads a text and no file, and `test/round-record.test.ts` hands one set of records to it and to the check's Python reader and compares what they return. This work adds fields to what the reader returns and changes no refusal, so that comparison stands.
- **The seal's records.** `src/daemon/seal-records.ts#readGuarantee` and `#readGate` read the two closing records' texts and no file. `seal-state.ts#sealAt` shows the form this work's reader follows: the directory found in the worktree by `decision-record.ts#workItemDirectory`, and every record read at the head.
- **Reading at a commit.** `worktrees.ts#fileAt(worktree, commit, path)` (line 1016) gives a file's text as a commit holds it, or null, and `#filesAt(worktree, commit, folder)` (line 1025) the files a commit holds in a folder. `#holdsCommit` (line 924) says whether one commit is another's ancestor.
- **The window.** A work item's view is the stage under a strip of tabs, and a right column 420px wide (`src/renderer/index.html:338` and `:673-712`). `work-item-view.ts#showView` (line 1064) draws the tabs from `view.ts#paneTabs` and takes the pane in front from `view.ts#stageOf`, and `enterView(workItemId, pane?)` (line 1112) names the pane in front. The pane in front is a pane's id, so a tab that is no pane is a state `showView` and `stageOf` do not hold today. `view.ts` holds the functions that compose what the renderer draws, and `test/view.test.ts` reads them with no window.
- **The body.** `src/daemon/github.ts#pullRequestBody` (line 459) composes a new pull request's body from a `BodyRecords`, and `#withSealSection` (line 488) replaces the seal section of an open one's body. `daemon.ts#runPullRequest` (lines 2706-2726) calls one or the other. The functions' cases stand in `test/pull-request.test.ts`, and the stage's cases in `test/chain.test.ts` and `test/pr-check.test.ts`.

The failure scenario of the chosen approach, in six months. The back joins two sources that only a stage's kind and round tie together. A stage that a later work item adds to the chain, as #56 adds the framer's and the crafter's, and that starts its session with no stage named, lands in the line of the sessions outside the chain, and its cost leaves the chain's sum without a failing case. The typed table of stage names (*Scope* item 11 of `frame/design.md`) fails the build for a new kind of `CHAIN_STAGE_KINDS`, which is where that work meets this one. The second weak point is the body's section: a person who edits a number inside the back's markers loses the edit at the next seal, and nothing tells them before.
