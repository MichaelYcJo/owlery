# Changelog: one press adds and names an account

Issue #218. Adding an account in the Accounts dialog took a typed name and a second press. "Add an account" stored an account under the name, and its row read "Sign-in not read yet" until the person pressed "Sign in" on it. A person who stopped after the first press kept an account that was not signed in.

- **"Add an account" opens Claude Code's sign-in at once, and the form holds no field.** The sign-in runs in the pane under the list, its head reads "Signing in to a new account", and "Add an account" is disabled while it runs.
- **The account is named from the login.** Its name is the email `claude auth status` prints for the login. Where the CLI names no email, an account Owlery made is named "Account" with its number, and the dialog says so and offers the row's "Rename". The person can rename any account afterwards, as before.
- **A sign-in that ends with no login leaves nothing.** A cancelled login, a closed pane and a login that failed each remove the folder Owlery made, and the dialog says "The sign-in ended with no login, so no account was added." A pane that ended by itself stays under the list until it is closed, so what the sign-in printed can be read. Where Claude Code could not say whether a login stands, the account is kept with its sign-in not read, and nothing is removed.
- **A second login to the same Claude account is kept, and its name tells it apart.** A name another account already holds takes a number in brackets, such as "person@example.com (2)". This holds for every add, so two accounts never share a name when they are added.
- **An account's row gives its name and its lines the dialog's width, and its buttons stand under them.** A row with many buttons used to narrow its name to a sliver, so an email broke inside the word and the usage lines wrapped every few words. The buttons now stand on a line of their own under the account's lines, and wrap where they do not fit.
- **"Use an existing folder…" takes no name either.** The account is named by the folder's login where one stands, and by the folder's last name otherwise. Owlery still makes, links and removes nothing in a folder it is handed.
- **A login that is still running when Owlery's background process stops is settled at its next start.** The process asks Claude Code of the folder, and keeps it as an account or removes it, as it does when the sign-in pane ends.
- **After an update, restart the background process before adding an account.** A background process of 0.0.4 that still runs refuses an add without a name in its own words, "An account is added with a name, and the absolute path of its folder where the person hands one over." "Restart Daemon…" in the menu ends it, and the app starts one of this version.
- **For a contributor:** `pnpm run screenshots` now also captures the Accounts dialog while the login of a new account runs, as `<theme>-<appearance>-accounts-adding.png`, and the dialog with the row that login named, as `<theme>-<appearance>-accounts-added.png`. The run's stand-in for the CLI holds and ends that login, and names it by an invented email, so no login of the person's is read and no browser opens.
