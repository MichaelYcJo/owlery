# Changelog: a stage a pane runs shows at work

Issue #223. In a Claude Code pane on the owl chain, the pane's own session runs the review rounds, the fix passes and the seal through Owlery's chain command. The approval line read only the work item's records and the daemon's own chain. So a role's place said "Not started" for the whole of a round the pane ran, often many minutes, and changed only once the round's record was committed.

- **A role's place is at work while a pane's session runs its stage.** When the chain command hands the session a round, a fix pass or the seal, the place of the proofer, the crafter or the sealer shows the working mark with "Round 1", "Fix pass" or "Judging", and the stage's age, as it does for a stage the daemon runs. While the chain command's broad gate waits for its turn on the machine or runs, the sealer's place says "Broad gate".
- **The place goes back to what the records say when the stage ends.** That happens once the chain command records the role's answer, once it hands no stage, and once the pane is closed or its process ends. A stage that a live pane's session leaves without calling the chain command again stays at work until the pane is closed or its process ends.
- **An open question and the daemon's own chain keep their place.** A question the role asks still marks its place waiting, and a stage the daemon's chain runs still says the place over a pane's stage.
- **For a contributor:** the chain command tells the daemon on its turn socket the stage `owed` hands and when it ends, with the pane's id from `OWLERY_PANE_ID`, and the daemon sends it to the window as `WorkItem.paneStage`. The daemon holds it in memory only. A daemon or a chain command of an earlier build works with the other as before, and the place then reads as it did.
