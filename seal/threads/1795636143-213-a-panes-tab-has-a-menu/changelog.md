# Changelog: a pane's tab has a menu that closes, renames and colours panes

Issue #213. A pane's tab had no menu of its own: a pane closed by its "×" or by ⌘W, one at a time, and a tab could be told from another only by its kind and its number.

- **A press of the right button on a pane's tab opens its menu.** It holds "Rename…", a row of colours, and under a rule "Close Pane", "Close Other Panes" and "Close Panes to the Right". "Close Other Panes" stands where the work item has another tab, and "Close Panes to the Right" where a tab stands after this one. The back's tab, "Back of the seal", opens no menu.
- **Closing several panes asks once.** Where none of the panes is working, they close at once. Where any is, one dialog names each working pane, says how many panes the close ends, and starts on "Cancel". Its button reads "Close pane" for one pane and "Close 3 panes" for three. The dialog closes and closes nothing where one of its panes is closed from elsewhere while it is open. "×", ⌘W and the pane's own "Close Pane" go through the same close, and a close of one working pane says what it said before.
- **"Rename…" gives a pane a name of its own.** A dialog holds the tab's label, selected, and takes 60 characters at most. The name then names the pane wherever the window names it by its tab's label: on its tab, in the close dialog, in the delete dialog and in a notification's title. The other panes of its kind keep their numbers. An empty name gives the pane its kind's label back.
- **The row's swatches mark a tab green, blue or pink.** A tab with a colour carries a bar 2px high along its bottom edge, the same shape for every colour, and its tooltip and its name to a screen reader end with the colour's word, as in "Shell 2, Blue". "No colour" takes the bar off. Each colour is a token of every theme, `--tab-green`, `--tab-blue` and `--tab-pink`, held at 3 to 1 or more against each ground the bar and the swatch stand on.
- **The daemon keeps a pane's name and colour as long as the pane.** They are set by a new message, `setPane`, and every window reads them in the next inbox, so they stay through a reload of the window, a change of the pane's account and the end of its process. They end with the pane, and so with the daemon: "Restart Daemon…" and an update that replaces an idle daemon end them as they end the pane.
- **A window of this build attached to a daemon of an earlier build** offers the close items alone, since that daemon keeps no name and no colour. A window of an earlier build attached to a daemon of this build ignores both.
